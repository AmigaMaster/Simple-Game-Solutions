# Hungry - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 25.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/hungry-2005)

## 1. Home

### 1.1. Bedroom

- PICK UP ging (int the bottom right of the shelf) (`Ging`)
- USE door

### 1.2. Hallway

- USE closed fridge
- PICK UP jar of nuts (`Jar of Nuts`)
- USE `Jar of Nuts` on `Ging` (`Loaded Ging`)
- USE door to street

## 2. Street

- USE mailbox (`Coupon`)
- USE food shop

### 2.1. Food Shop

- TALK TO checkout
- EXIT food shop

## 3. Street

- EXIT north into the alley

## 4. Alley

- USE `Loaded Ging` on bin
  - CLICK on the rat
- USE bin (`Deoderant`)

## 5. Street

- TALK TO Bobbs
- USE `Deoderant` on Bobbs
- USE door to house of porcelain

### 5.1. House of Porcelain

- USE `Coupon` on potter (`Mug`)
- TALK TO potter
- USE door to street

## 6. Street

- EXIT north (above the crosswalk)

## 7. Northern Street

- TALK TO Hobo
- USE `Mug` on Hobo
- PICK UP spoon (a small hotspot in the wind chime) (`Spoon`)
- USE door to sports shop

### 7.1. Sports Shop

- TALK TO Sporta (`Milk`)
- USE door to street

## 8. Northern Street

- EXIT south

## 9. Street

### 9.1. Food Shop

- PICK UP cereal `Cereal`
- LOOK AT `Cereal` (`Frisbee`)

## 10. Street

### 10.1. House of Porcelain

- USE `Frisbee` on bowl (on the right table) (`Bowl`)

## 11. Home

### 11.1. Hallway

- USE `Spoon` on sink (`Clean Spoon`)
- USE `Cereal` on `Bowl` (`Bowl of Cereal`)
- USE `Milk` on `Bowl of Cereal` (`Breakfast`)
- USE `Clean Spoon` on `Breakfast`
