## 44. Balloon

- LOOK AT round instrument

- USE hamper
- PICK UP tins **2 times** (`Two tins of rhubarb paste`)
- LOOK AT long instrument (Altimeter)

  - **NOTE:** In the current game version, the charts have an error. AT 1500 feet, in row 4, the wind to the east stops at 3east!
    - There are 3 charts in total
    - Each of the chart is for a different altitude (500, 1000, 1500)
    - The patterns show the wind directions at the altitude
    - There are coordinate numbers at the top and left
    - You need to travel to the destination at coordinate 6,6
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Get to the East End
  >- ROUTE (we start at 500 feet / coordinate 2s,2e)
  >   1. USE `Two tins of rhubarb paste` with sky
  >      - Drop both, (1500 feet / 2s,1e)
  >   2. USE knot (on the balloon)
  >      - 2 seconds (500 feet / 4s,1e)
  >   3. USE 2 tin of rhubarb paste with sky (1500 feet / 4s,3e)
  >   4. USE knot for 1 second (1000 feet / 6s,3e)
  >   5. USE knot for 1 second (500 feet / 6s,3e)
  >   6. USE 1 tin of rhubarb with sky (1000 / 6s,3e)
  >   7. USE knot for 1 second (500 feet / 6s,6e)
