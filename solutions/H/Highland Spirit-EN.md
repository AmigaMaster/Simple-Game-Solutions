## 1. Ploc Moor Lodge

### 1.1. Outside Cottage

- LOOK AT plaque
- LOOK AT `Phone`
- LOOK AT wall
- USE wall (`Cottage key`)
- USE `Cottage key` with door

#### 1.1.1. Cottage

- USE drawers (`Map`)
- USE bag (`Knife`)
- EXIT south

### 1.2. Outside Cottage

- EXIT west

### 1.3. Moor Entrance

- LOOK AT Information board
- EXIT west

### 1.4. Outside Cottage

- USE car

## 2. Ploc Kirk

### 2.1. Outside Church

- TAKE spade (`Spade`)
- LOOK AT (2nd gravestone in the back row from the left, Elizabeth McRae)
- EXIT east

## 3. Ploc Library

### 3.1. Outside Library

- USE box (`Damp book`)
- USE door

#### 3.1.1. Library

- USE books (in the back shelf at the lower right)
- USE door

### 3.2. Outside Library

- USE car

## 4. Shops

### 4.1. Outside Shops

- LOOK AT (left) notice (inside the notices board)
- USE (green) door

### 4.2. Aladdin's Cave

- USE poster
- TAKE metal detector
- LOOK AT banner (in front of the counter)
- LOOK AT rock (on the ground right next to the fish)
- USE `Knife` with rock
- TALK TO proprietor
  - Ask about the banner
    - **NOTE:** This dialogue option is only available when you looked at the banner
- EXIT south

### 4.3. Outside Shops

- USE car

## 5. The Stuart Arms

### 5.1. Outside Pub

- USE door

#### 5.1.1. Pub

- LOOK AT book
- LOOK AT parchment
- TALK TO landlord
  - Ask for feather
  - Ask for the book
- TALK TO patron
- TAKE taster pot (from the counter) **4 times** (`Meathead taster pot`, `MacLeish taster pot`, `Midnight taster pot`, `Mocassin taster pot`)
- USE `Midnight taster pot` with patron
- TAKE pencil (`Pencil`)
- USE remote control
- TAKE display case (`Feather`)
- LOOK AT painting (the most right one)
  - **NOTE:** It's Lord Long Lachlan
- USE door

##### 5.1.1.1. Toilets

- LOOK AT poster
- TAKE toilet paper dispenser (`Toilet paper`)
- USE `Pencil` with `Toilet paper` (`Tracing kit`)
- USE `Tracing kit` with poster (`Traced signature`)
- USE `Damp book` with dryer (`Book`)
- USE `Traced signature` with `Book` (`Signed book`)
- EXIT south

#### 5.1.2. Pub

- EXIT south

### 5.2. Outside Pub

- USE car

## 8. Glenblae

## 9. Ploc Library

### 9.1. Outside Library

#### 9.1.1. Library

- USE books (in the third row in the left shelf, book about Scottish history)
- LOOK AT **all ten** plaques
  - **NOTE:** At the violet one, there must be a comment about Lord Mackenzie. If not, LOOK AT the most right picture in the pub.

## 12. Ploc Moor Lodge

### 12.1. Stream

- TALK TO gentleman
  - "I know where you ancestors might have been!"
- USE `Metal detector` with another random patch of ground (located in the lower left part, at the height of the left root of the third tree)
- USE `Spade` with another random patch of ground (`Signet ring`)

### 12.2. Outside Cottage

#### 12.2.1. Cottage

- USE `Signed ring` with bed
- USE `Feather` with bed
