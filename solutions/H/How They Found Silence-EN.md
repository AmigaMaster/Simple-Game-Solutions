## 28. Fern Manor

### 28.1. First Floor

- USE (left) door

#### 28.1.1. Lord Fern's Room

- USE (top tight) drawer
  - **NOTE:** Combination `A-5-U-3-IV`
- TALK TO Kara

### 28.2. Library

- USE the following books:
  1. Bottom shelf, the "A"
  2. Green "5"
  3. Blue "U"
  4. Green "3"
  5. The one with the Roman "IV"
- ENTER huge door

#### 28.2.1. Laboratory

- USE thick book (to the right of the skull)
  - **NOTE:** The recipe:
    - 12 drops of salamander blood
    - 1 dragon eye
    - Kalamancus powder
    - Unicorn's heart
- TALK TO Kara
  - "Do you know anything about these ingredients?"
    - **NOTE:** Kalamancus powder is green
- USE page (to the left of the skull)
  - **NOTE:** The Unicorn's heart has been swapped with the golden frog
- PICK UP
  - vial (the red one in the bottom shelf)
  - red eyeball (right object on the second shelf)
  - green powder (bottom shelf)
  - gold powder
  - sticks
- EXIT west

### 28.3. Library

- "No, I'm trying to find the stone."
- "To change Kara back into one of you."
