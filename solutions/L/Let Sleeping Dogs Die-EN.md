| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|

Download the free game from [itch.io](https://branegames.itch.io/let-sleeping-dogs-lie)

## 1. PDA

- CLICK on the bottom icon
- Inbox
  - ACCEPT TRANSMISSION
  - ATTEMPT RESTORE
  - READ **all five** parts and CLICK on the three highlighted texts
    - Buzzati Lake
    - Kayden Tower
    - Privacy Hotel
  - **NOTE:** Time of the call was 20:52
  - SELECT megaphone tab on the bottom
- News
  - CLICK **all five** highlighted texts
    - Techne Industries
    - Techne T
    - Daniel Drinkwater
    - Mark Vardy
    - Robert Nowak
  - SELECT map icon
- City Map
  - Sights
    - **NOTE:** Buzzati Lake location
  - SELECT Restaurants
- Restaurants
  - **NOTE:** Drogo Bar is near Buzzati Lake
  - SELECT Hotels
- Hotels
  - **NOTE:** Breach Inn is near Techne T
  - SELECT police badge
- Police System
  - DB Search
    - Robert Nowak
  - Incident Record
    - Caller Identity: Robert Nowak
    - Caller Location: Breach Inn
    - Incident Location: Drogo Bar
    - Incident Classification: Kidnapping
    - SUBMIT

## 2. Hotel

### 2.1. Lobby

- USE Tablet
  - Police System
    - Hack Force
      - HACK SYSTEM
      - Reservations Log
        - **NOTE:** The time of the call was 20:52, so the check-in must be the one from 20:47, Room 303
      - SELECT Key Cutting
      - Key Cutting
        - Floor 3
        - Room 3
        - CUT KEY
        - First Name Initial: R(obert)
        - Last Name Initial: N(owak)
      - EXIT PDA
- USE elevator

### 2.2. Room

- LOOK AT footprints
  - **NOTE:** 6'5" height
- LOOK AT hat
  - **NOTE:** Long black hairs
- LOOK AT body
  - LOOK AT neck
    - **NOTE:** Impressions of ten fingers
  - LOOK AT shirt pocket
  - LOOK AT hand
- USE Pager (on the ground)
  - Police System
    - Hack Force
      - HACK SYSTEM
      - Sent
        - LISTEN
          - **NOTE:** "He" ist coming back
      - Received
        - **NOTE:** A man in the 30s
      - SELECT Crime Scene
    - Crime Scene
      - Victim Sheet
        - Name: Robert Nowak
        - Occupation: Journalist
        - Cause of death: Asphyxiation
        - Location: Hotel Breach Inn
        - Approximate time of incident: ~21:00
        - SUBMIT
      - Perpetrator Sheet
        - Sex: Male
        - Race: Caucasian
        - Hair length: Long
        - Hair color: Black
        - Height [Estimated]: Up to 200cm (6'7")
        - Age [Estimated]: 26-35
        - SUBMIT
