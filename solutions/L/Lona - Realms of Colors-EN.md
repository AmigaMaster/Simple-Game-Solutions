# Lona - Realms of Colors - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 04.09.2022 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

## 1. Level 1

- FIND 3 blue drawn papers and attach them to the picture to the right:
  - 2x left the floor
  - 1x inside the drawer on the left
- USE crow
- USE door
- USE cat
- OPEN blue color pot beside the drawing you have created above
- USE blue color pot
- USE coffee mug
- USE crow

## 2. Level 2

- USE lampshade several times
- USE umbrella next to the tree to your left
- USE 3 candles under the umbrella
- USE cat
- USE flower on the stone at the tree **3 times**
- SORT the colors of the lights inside the sculptures to the right
- TAKE turquoise colored light from the hand on the left and put it into the sculpture to the right
- USE crow
- USE 3 lampshades on the right
- USE lampshade several times until it begins to fly away

## 3. Level 3

- USE books in the shelf on the wall to the right to access the key
- USE key with lock on the door
- USE cat
- USE 3 small wind wheels on the left
- USE 7 flowers in your hair to get access to the key
- USE key on door
- USE big wind wheel on crow (water wheel)
- USE water wheel on middle fountain level
- USE lever
- USE small lever inside the shelf
- USE cat
- USE 4 audio cassettes
- TAKE clef und USE it on the crow (transforms into audio cassette)
- USE audio cassette on cassette player

## 4. Level 4

- USE Monitor
- USE 2 packages on the floor in front of the door
- USE skateboard from the package with window handle
- USE window handle with left window
- USE window handle to open window
- USE cat
- Flying things:
  - USE notes when they are above the lower right card box
  - USE sun when it is beside the upper left card box
  - USE papers when they are next to the upper right card box
  - USE cloud when it is next to the lower left card box
- TAKE each of the card boxes and use them with the crow, then PUT them on the ground and get the content. To change views, use the cat:
  - USE notes right next to the card box on the right wall
  - USE drawing equipment with table next to the cat
  - USE lamp with table above the cat
  - USE bedclothes with bed
- USE cat
- USE card box with chair

## 5. Level 5

- USE 3 bells on the left
- USE cat
- USE each of the four ice lollies in the background **3 times**
- TAKE all four ice pillars
- USE ice pillar with crow in the background
- CUT 3 benches on the tree to the left
- SET all 6 clocks:
  1. top right
  2. right
  3. top right
  4. right
  5. top right
  6. south, southwest
- TAKE key from the bridge
- USE key with chest
- TAKE golden ball
- USE cat
- USE golden ball with traffic light
- USE traffic light
- TAKE key
- USE key with crow
- USE key on lock

## 6. Level 6

- OPEN bag to your feet
- USE window (nothing happens)
- TAKE piece of paper from the bag
- USE piece of paper on window
- USE cat
- USE green, red and blue cone with monster left to the cones
- TAKE blue cone from the bench on the left
- USE cat
- USE blue cone on blue balloon
- USE crow on the window to your right
  - Repeat this for the red and yellow cone

## 7. Level 7

- TAKE torch
- USE torch on 3 lamps
- USE car
- USE pencils on the crow:
  1. 5th pencil
  2. 4th pencil
  3. 4th pencil
  4. 8th pencil
  5. 5th pencil
  6. 2nd pencil
- TAKE piece of paper on the left
- USE piece of paper on the car
- TAKE piece of paper behind the crow
- USE piece of paper on the car
- USE spinning thing above your head
- TAKE black spinner
- USE black spinner on angel
- TAKE white spinner
- USE white spinner on lamps
