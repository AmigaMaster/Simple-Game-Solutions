# Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 31.10.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [itch.io](https://bbgames-it.itch.io/indiana-jones-and-the-temple-of-doom-unofficial)

## 1. Club Obi Wan

- PICK UP Flambé pigeon skewer (at the piano on the background) (`Flambé pigeon skewer`)
- USE `Flambé pigeon skewer` with Chen
- USE ice buckets
- PUSH service cart (the cart on the right)
- PICK UP antidote vial (from the table on the left)
- PICK UP warrior's sword (from the statue) (`Warrior's sword`)
- USE `Warrior's sword` with Gong rope
- PUSH gong
- USE big window

## 2. Escape in the Duesenberg

- PICK UP shoulder bag (on the car) (`Shoulder bag`)
- LOOK AT `Shoulder bag` (`Revolver`)
- TALK TO Willie (`Antidote vial`)
- USE `Antidote vial`
- TALK TO Short Round
- USE `Revolver` with Lao's sedan

## 3. Drive to Airport

- east, north **3 times**, east, north, east, south, east, south, east **3 times**, north, east, north, east

## 4. Airport

- TALK TO Copilot
- USE `Revolver` with chicken crates
- TALK TO Short Round
- TALK TO Copilot

## 5. Airplane

- PICK UP fire extinguisher (`Fire extinguisher`)
- USE `Fire extinguisher` with Indy
- PICK UP whip (`Whip`)
- OPEN cockpit
  - SHOOT the airplanes
- OPEN cargo trunk
- PICK UP emergency raft (`Emergency raft`)
- USE `Emergency raft`on cargo hatch

## 6. Mayapore Village

- TALK TO Shaman
- TALK TO Willie
- ENTER guest hut (`Scraps of painted cloth`)
- USE `Scraps of painted cloth` with Indy
  - Complete the puzzle, its very easy (`Painted cloth`)
- USE `Painted cloth` with Shaman
- USE `Painted cloth` with elephant
- TALK TO village Chief
- TALK TO Shaman
  - "Pankot?"
- EXIT northeast onto the path to Pankot
- TALK TO Willie
  - "Your earrings..."
  - "Then you can walk..." (`Willie's earrings`)
- USE `Willie's earrings` with village Chief (`Sugarcane`)
- USE `Sugarcane` with elephant
- EXIT northeast onto the path to Pankot

## 7. Jungle

- TALK TO Sajnu
- TALK TO Short Round
  - "Shorty, come with me."
- PICK UP dry branch (in the middle of the jungle at the clearing) (`Resinous branch`)
- ENTER hut

### 7.1. Hut

- PICK UP yellow handkerchief (`Yellow handkerchief`)
- USE `Resinous branch` on brazier (`Torch`)
- EXIT west

## 8. Jungle

- WALK south a little bit west of the plank footbridge (you can walk through the jungle) to cross the bridge
- ENTER ruined shrine

### 8.1. Ruined Shrine

- EXIT east

### 8.2. Ruins

- ENTER waterfall

#### 8.2.1. Hidden Cave

- LOOK AT carved hand (`Notebook`)
- PICK UP abandoned camp (New note: Prof. E. Whitmore)
- EXIT west

### 8.3. Ruins

- TALK TO Short Round
  - "Think you can climb..."
- USE `Whip` with log
- EXIT northeast

## 9. Jungle

- TALK TO Sajnu

## 10. River Camp

- LOOK AT elephants
- USE `Whip` with river
- TALK TO Willie
  - "Calm it down: scratch it gently behind the head..."
- PICK UP dry branches (`Dry branches`)
- USE `Dry branches` with campfire
- TALK TO Willie
- USE blanket

## 11. Jungle Clearing

- LOOK AT statue of Kali
  - **NOTE:** The hands of the statue from "Sky to Earth"
    1. Sword
    2. Hangman's noose
    3. Trident
    4. Head
- EXIT north (path to the palace)
- LOOK AT footprints (at the bottom of the screen right to the base of the statue)
- WALK to vines (right next to the head in the hand of the statue)

## 12. Rock Face

- USE the four stone rollers and select the symbols `Earth, Sword, Hangman's noose, Trident`
- ENTER stone door

## 13. Palace

### 13.1. Fountain

- TALK TO Chatter Lal
- LOOK AT guest book
  - **NOTE:** 18.03.1931 Prof. Edward Whitmore, Oxford
- TALK TO Chatter Lal
  - "Professor Whitmore was your guest, back in '31."
- LOOK AT shoulder bag (`Tuxedo`)
- USE `Tuxedo` with fountain
- USE `Tuxedo` with oil lamps
- USE `Tuxedo` with Indy
- EXIT north

### 13.2. Hallway

- LOOK AT display case of sacred objects (on the left side above the blue pillow)
- TALK TO Captain Blumburtt
- TALK TO Chatter Lal
- PICK UP bread basket (`Bread and fruit`)
- GIVE `Bread and fruit` to Willie
- TALK TO Chatter Lal
- USE `Whip` with candle on the pedestal

### 13.3. Bedroom

- PICK UP whip (`Whip`)
- PICK UP candle (`Candle`)
- PICK UP ceremonial sword (`Ceremonial sword`)
  - **ATTENTION:** If you forgot to pick up the sword, you can't finish the game
- EXIT through the door
- PICK UP brass vase
- USE `Whip` with Assassin
- PULL fan lever

### 13.4. Willie's Room

- USE `Candle` with giant bug
- TALK TO Short Round
- EXIT north

### 13.5. Hallway

- OPEN display case of sacred objects
- TALK TO Short Round
- USE `Ceremonial sword` with display case of sacred objects (`Ivory tusk`)

### 13.6. Willie's Room

- USE `Ivory tusk` with Ganesh statuette
- PUSH Ganesh statuette
- ENTER secret passage

### 13.7. Passage

- EXIT east

### 13.8. Stone Room

- PUSH protruding brick
- OPEN door

### 13.9. Willie's Room

- ENTER secret passage

### 13.10. Passage

- USE oil lamp
- PUSH loose stone
- USE hole in the wall **2 times**
  - CLICK on the bugs and pick up all chains, bones, etc. until you can reach the ring at the top left

### 13.11. Stone Room

- PUSH spiral stone
- EXIT north

### 13.12. Catacombs

- WALK south, east, north

#### 13.12.1. Workshop

- PICK UP statuette with a hat (`Clay figurine`)
- PICK UP clay tub (`Clay tub`)
- PICK UP torn page
- LOOK AT `Notebook`
- EXIT northwest

### 13.13. Catacombs

- WALK south, west, south, east, south, follow the path

#### 13.13.1. Ossuary

- LOOK AT stele
- PICK UP loose stone (at the bottom left of the stele)
- EXIT west

### 13.14. Catacombs

- WALK east, north, east, south and follow the path
- LOOK AT crack in the rock
- TALK TO Short Round
  - "Can you fit through that crack?"
    - **NOTE:** You need to walk around to be able to click on Short Round
- TALK TO Willie
  - "Willie, give Shorty the lamp."

#### 13.14.1. Bell Chamber

- PULL bell rope
- EXIT west

### 13.15. Catacombs

- USE stone wheel
  - From top to bottom `Moon, Skull, Water lily`
- EXIT east

### 13.16. Tunnel Mouth

- WALK TO carved column (the pillar on the right)

### 13.17. Temple of Doom

- USE `Whip` with stone elephant (at the top of the screen)
- PULL chain winch (`Iron crank`)
- ENTER passage behind the altar

### 13.18. Above Mine Shaft

- PICK UP stones (on the ground) (`Pebble`)
- USE USE `Pebble` with mine shaft
- PICK UP thuggee robe (`Thuggee robe`)
- USE `Thuggee robe` with Indy
- USE `Iron crank` with winch
- PUSH boulder

### 13.19. Mine

- PICK UP nail (in the wooden prop)
- TALK TO child
  - "I need that big brute to start making noise."
- PICK UP nail (`Nail`)
- USE `Nail` with chain ring (the big one on he ground)
- PICK UP shovel (`Shovel`)
- USE `Shovel` with Thuggee guard
- PICK UP torch
- EXIT east

### 13.20. Temple of Doom

- USE `Torch` with Indy

### 13.21. Above Mine Shaft

- PICK UP winch (`Iron crank`)

### 13.22. Temple of Doom

- USE `Iron crank` with chain winch
- PICK UP Sankara stones (`Sankara stones`)
- PICK UP chain winch (`Iron crank`)
- EXIT east

### 13.23. Above Mine Shaft

- USE `Iron crank` on winch
- USE `Thuggee robe` with `Ceremonial sword` (`Strips of cloth`)
- USE `Strips of cloth` with chain

### 13.24. Mine Car Tunnel

- USE mine car

### 13.25. Rails

- North, **2 times**, east **4 times**

## 14. Rope Bridge

- USE `Whip` with bridge ropes
- USE `Ceremonial sword` with bridge ropes
- PUSH rock face
- PICK UP Sankara stone
- USE `Sankara stone` with Thuggee guard
- CLIMB up
