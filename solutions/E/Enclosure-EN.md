## 2. Day 1

### 2.1. Helicopter

- Use the cursor keys to land the helicopter

### 2.2. Your Room

- Walk to the right into the shower
- TAKE SHOWER (1/1)
- TAKE CLOTHES from table (1/2)
- USE COMPUTER (1/3)
- EXIT south

### 2.3. Hallways

- Exit north, east

### 2.4. Bar

- SIT on the most right chair
- TALK TO OWEN **2 times** (3/6)
- TALK TO LISA **3 times** (3/9)
- STAND
- Exit east

### 2.5. Kitchen

- Exit south

### 2.6. Pantry

- LOOK SHELVES and walk to the back shelf (7/16)
- TAKE MATCHES (`box of matches`)
- Exit west **3 times**

### 2.7. Hallways

- Exit south, south, south, south,

### 2.8. Control Room

- TALK TO FRANK **2 times**
- Exit west, west

### 2.9. Hallway

- Exit west

### 2.10. Hallway

- LOOK STAINS
- Exit north, north, north, north, east, east

### 2.11. Kitchen

- TALK TO SARAH **7 times** (3/19)
- Exit west, west, southwest, south

### 2.12. Hallway

- Enter lower right door

### 2.13. Ben Green's Room

- TALK TO BEN **5 times** (6/25)
- Exit south, south, south, southeast, east, east, east, south

### 2.14. Small Room

- TAKE ROD (`fishing rod`) (7/32)
- CLIMB ladder

### 2.15. Watch Tower

- TALK TO LEE **5 times** (3/35)
- CLIMB
- Exit east, west, west, west, west, north, north east

## 5. Day 4

### 5.1. Watch Tower

- CLIMB

### 5.2. Hallway

- Exit east, west, west

### 5.3. Hallway

- TALK TO BENSON **4 times** (3/113)
- USE COOLER (1/114)
- Exit north, northeast

### 5.4. Doctor's Room

- TALK TO SARAH **4 times** (`couplet`) (8/122)
- READ COUPLET
- Exit west, southeast

### 5.5. Lab

- WEAR SUIT
- Exit east

### 5.6. Test Lab

- Walk to the thing on the floor
- Re-enter the test lab
- GET CARD (`keycard`) (8/130)
- Exit west

### 5.7. Lab

- TAKE OFF SUIT
- Exit west, south, east, east, south

### 5.8. Hallway

- USE CARD with the door to the north
- OPEN DOOR to the north
- Exit north

### 5.9. Garage

- LOOK BOX in the lower-right corner
- GET DYNAMITE (`dynamite`) (8/138)
- Exit west, east, west, west, north, north, north

### 5.10. Main Entrance

- WEAR CLOTHES
- OPEN DOOR to the north

### 5.11. In Front of the Station

- Exit west

### 5.12. Frozen Lake

- LOOK STONE
- USE DYNAMITE WITH HOLE (2/140)
- LIGHT DYNAMITE
- Exit west

### 5.13. Cliff

- **ATTENTION:** You need to walk very closely to the flowers, pixel by pixel until the cliff begins to decent
- GET FLOWER (8/148)
- Exit east, east

### 5.14. In Front of the Station

- OPEN DOOR and enter the station

### 5.15. Main Entrance

- TAKE OFF CLOTHES
- Exit southwest, south, southeast

### 5.16. Ben Green's Room

- **ATTENTION:** Do not walk near the plants (8/156)
- GIVE FLOWER TO BEN
- Carefully walk to the tree in the top left corner
- GET FRUIT (`piece of fruit`) (8/164)
- Exit south, south, south, southeast

### 5.17. Hallway

- LOOK STAINS
- Exit west, north, north, east

### 5.18. Ben Green's Room

- **ATTENTION:** Do not move!
- RUB FRUIT ON BODY (8/172)
- GET GLASSES (`spectangle lens`) (8/180)
- Exit south, north, northeast

### 5.19. Owen's Room

- USE LENSE WITH TELESCOPE (7/187)
- USE TELESCOPE
  - Move the telescope a little bit down and then to the left. You will see something flashing
  - CTRL+C
- Exit south, north, southeast, south, northeast

### 5.20. Doctor's Room

- TALK TO OWEN **3 times** (3/190)
- Exit west, north, north, east

### 5.21. Bar

- Exit south

### 5.22. Restroom

- MOVE MIRROR
  - Y to get the key (`ignition key`) (7/197)
- Exit south, east, south, south, south, south

### 5.23. Hallway

- OPEN DOOR to the north
- Exit north

### 5.24. Garage

- **ATTENTION:** Save the game
- TALK TO FRANK **4 times**

### 5.25. Crash Site

- Walk to the right, just before you begin to walk behind the snow wall
- USE PICKAXE ON ICE
- Exit north

### 5.26. Cave

- Walk to the woman sitting on the right
- READ JOURNAL (2/199)
- Walk to the right-most scooter
- SEARCH SCOOTER (`carburettor`) (8/207)
- Exit west

### 5.27. Crash Site

- GIVE CARBURETTOR TO FRANK (2/209)

### 5.28. Garage

- Exit west, east, west, west, south

### 5.29. Control Room

- LOOK SCREEN
- Exit west, north, northeast

### 5.30. Doctor's Room

- Walk to the right
- GET SCALPEL (`scalpel`) (8/217)
- Exit west

### 5.31. Hallway

- OPEN PANEL
- USE SCALPEL ON WIRES (2/219)
- Exit north, north, east, south

### 5.32. Restroom

- TALK TO LISA **4 times**
- Exit south, east

### 5.33. Kitchen

- GET CROWBAR (`crowbar`) (2/221)
- Exit west, west, south-east, south, south, east, east, south

### 5.34. Hallway

- OPEN DOOR to the north
- Exit north

### 5.35. Garage

- Exit west, east, west, west, west

### 5.36. Hallway with Shaft

- OPEN SHAFT WITH CROWBAR
- **ATTENTION:** Save the game
- CLIMB

### 5.37. Roof

- **QUICK:** Walk to the right side of the shaft to hide here
- Walk to the left and find your way without being detected
  - Walk to the upper-right cover
  - Wait for the satellite dish to cover Owen and **IMMEDIATELY** walk as close to the wall to the cover to the black grate
  - Walk as far as possible right from the grate
  - Wait for the dish to faced the camera **AND** Owen looked back **TWO** times, quickly push the bottom-left diagonal key to walk to the satellite dish. **YOU ARE NOT SAVE HERE**
  - Wait until Owen looked back **TWO** times.
  - **IMMEDIATELY** walk to left-most cover and do not fall from the roof at the lower side.
  - In front of the cover, walk towards Owen
- **QUICK:** Walk to the pipe, again, do not fall from the roof
- **QUICK:** USE CROWBAR ON PIPE
