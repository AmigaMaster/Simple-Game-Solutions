# Shadows Of Serenity - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 05.02.2025 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download or play the game from the [itch.io homepage](https://wosiee.itch.io/shadows-of-serenity)

## 1. Office

- USE desk lamp
- TAKE letter (on the desk below the lamp) (`Letter`)
- USE drawer (`Key`)
- USE `Key` with Wall Safe
- OPEN Wall Safe
  - TAKE Pistol (`Pistol`)
  - TAKE Forensic Kit (`Forensic Kit`)
  - TAKE badge (`Badge`)
- LOOK AT photo
  - LOOK AT all three persons
- USE terminal **2 times**
  - OPEN all files
- LEAVE Office

## 2. Street

- TALK TO Street dweller
- EXIT south

## 3. Construction Site

- LOOK AT unconscious worker
- TAKE torn jacket (the green one in the middle of the screen) (`Lockpicks`)
- TAKE bags of concrete (`Bag of concrete`)
- EXIT south

## 4. Street

- USE `Lockpicks` with car
- USE `Forensic Kit` with footprint (on the ground in front ot the left door)
- USE `Bag of concrete` with footprint (`Perfect mold`)
- USE `Forensic Kit` with `Perfect mold`
- TALK TO Street dweller
  - "Shadow"
  - "Serial number"

## 5. Office

- USE terminal
  - OPEN Serial number
  - OPEN Shadow
  - OPEN Olivia

## 6. Takahashi Office

- TALK TO receptionist

## 7. Theater

- LOOK AT door
  - OPEN entrance door
  - ENTER entrance door

### 7.1. Entrance Hall

- ENTER Toilets

### 7.2. Toilets

- USE lid (`Pipe1`, `Pipe2`, `Pipe3`, `Pipe4`)
- USE cabinet
  - USE `Pipe2` with upper plumbing
  - USE `Pipe1` with left plumbing
  - USE `Pipe3` with right plumbing
  - USE `Pipe4` with lower plumbing
  - USE Valve **2 times**
- EXIT south

### 7.3. Entrance Hall

- LOOK AT Keypad
  - TAKE Chewing Gum (`Chewing Gum`)

### 7.4. Toilets

- LOOK AT cabinet
  - USE `Chewing Gum` with right plumbing
- USE faucet
- LOOK AT Mirror
  - **NOTE:** `IT51`

### 7.5. Entrance Hall

- USE Keypad
  - ENTER `IT51` (Last button in 3rd row, 2nd button in 1st row, 1st button in 5th row, 1st button in 3rd row)
- ENTER stage

#### 7.5.1. Stage

- USE `Pistol` with all targets
- TAKE Disc (`Disc`)
- USE Ethan (`Bullet`)
- EXIT west through the door

## 8. Commercial Districy

- EXIT north
