# Stately Groan - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 08.08.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Get free game from [Gamejolt](https://gamejolt.com/games/statelygroan/421403)

## 1. Front Grounds

- LOOK AT `Guide to Diddling`
- WALK west

## 2. Tea Room

- TALK TO Penelope
- TALK TO Manager
- WALK east
- **NOTES:**
  - The people are walking around in the houses, you meet them at different locations
  - In some rooms a character named Nancy will ask you some questions. You need to answer them incorrect to progress in the game

## 3. Entryway

- TALK TO Kevin
  - **NOTE:** You need to talk to Kevin here in the entryway, just keep waiting until he walk to you
  - "Small urn?" (He moves the urn)
- TALK TO Chelsea (`Bubble Gum`)
- TALK TO Sandra
- WALK east

## 4. Great Hall

- WALK east

## 5. Kitchen / Pantry

- USE small urn
- TALK TO Mr. Tonaka
- TALK TO Judith
  - "Drink?" (`Plastic Straws`)
- TALK TO Lauren
- OPEN cupboard
- PICK UP trophy (`Walrus Trophy`)
- PICK UP sieve (above the cupboard) (`Sieve`)
- WALK east

## 6. Garden

- USE urn (you cannot move it)
- TALK TO Kevin
  - "Medium urn?"
- USE (third from the left) soil mound (`Music Piece`)

## 7. Great Hall

- EXAMINE urn
- TALK TO Kevin
  - "Big urn?" (`Boar Trophy`)
- GIVE `Bubble Gum` to Lauren (`Apple`)
- EXIT upstairs to the left

## 8. Guest Bedroom

- OPEN curtain
- PICK UP chamber pot (`Chamber Pot`)
- PICK UP sewing shears (from the table in front of the mirror) (`Sewing Shears`)
- WALK east

## 9. Dining Room

- Nancy's question:
  - No
- TALK TO Nancy
- TALK TO Buck
- USE `Plastic Straws` with dumb waiter
- WALK east

## 10. Drawing Room

- PICK UP book (from the top right in the shelf) (`Illegible Book`)

## 11. Kitchen / Pantry

- USE `Chamber Pot` with drain

## 12. Dining Room

- PICK UP wine bottle (`Wine`)
- USE `Wine` with dumb waiter

## 13. Kitchen / Pantry

- PICK UP chamber pot (`Pot of Wine`)
- EXIT upstairs **2 times** and to the east

## 14. Library

- USE shelf (the red book in the left shelf in the upper side) (`War and Peace`)
- USE shelf (the green book on the right upper shelf) (`Reasonable Doubt`)
- WALK east

## 15. Billiard Room

- Nancy's question:
  - Yes
- USE wall (`Elk Trophy`)
- PICK UP billiard cues (`Billiard Cue`)
- EXIT west **all the way**

## 16. Master Bedroom

- Nancy's question:
  - Elizabethan (after answering three questions wrong, you get their ticket and they walk to the gift shop) (`Ticket`)
- USE pillow (`Discourage Your Hound`)
- EXAMINE wardrobe
- USE `Billiard Cue` with wardrobe (`Music Piece`)
- TALK TO Mr. Tonaka
  - "Billiard room?" (follow them to the billiard room)

## 17. Billiard Room

- TALK TO Mr. Tonaka
  - "Test floorboards?"

## 18. Drawing Room

- PICK UP pheasant trophy (`Pheasant Trophy`)

## 19. Billiard Room

- USE **all four*** trophies on the frames on the wall in the following order (from left to right):
  1. `Pheasant Trophy`
  2. `Walrus Trophy`
  3. `Elk Trophy`
  4. `Boar Trophy`

## 20. Front Grounds

- USE `Sieve` with front lawn (`Heart Pendant`)

## 21. Tea Room

- USE `Billiard Cue` with tea caddy (on the wall above the coffee machine) (`Tea Caddy`)
- GIVE `Ticket` to Penelope
- WALK west

### 21.1. Gift Shop

- USE `Sewing Shears` on Buck (`Loose Change`)
- PICK UP book (in the shelf a to the left of the pink shirt) (`A Story of Hope`)

## 22. Garden

- USE the following items an the soil mounds(from left to right):
  1. `Pot of Wine`
  2. `Heart Pendant`
  3. `Apple`
  4. `Tea Caddy`

## 23. Library

- TALK TO Sandra
  - "Recital?"
- EXAMINE glass case
- TALK TO Penelope
  - "Have book?" (`Her Last Promise`)
  - "Dickens?"
    - **NOTE:** Love her against `Reason, promise, peace, hope, happiness, discouragement`
- USE the following books with the empty spaces in the shelves, from left to right:
  1. `Reasonable Doubt`
  2. `Last Promise`
  3. `War and Peace`
  4. `A Story of Hope`
  5. `Illegible Book`
  6. `Discourage your Hound`
- EXIT west

## 24. Guest Bedroom

- TALK TO Judith
  - "Recital?"

## 25. Great Hall

- TALK TO Chelsea
  - "Recital?"

## 26. Drawing Room

- USE **both** `Music Piece` with piano
- EXAMINE piano
  - **NOTE:** `A E G B`
- USE piano
  - "Play the notes A E G B"
- TALK TO Sandra
  - "Hologram?"
- EXIT to the top floor

## 27. Long Gallery

- USE ladder
