### 72.2. First Floor

- ENTER door

#### 72.2.1. Hallway

- >ACHIEVEMENT: Straight on Till Morning
  >- **NOTE:** You need to solve the door puzzle without asking for help
  >- The logic is a literal translation of Peter Pan's famous directions to Neverland: "Second star to the right, and straight on 'til morning"
  >- Note the stars in the background image and count them
  >- Find the second star, then enter the door immediately to its right
  >   1. Floor: Fourth door
  >   2. Floor: Fifth door
  >   3. Floor: Second door
  >   4. Floor: Third door
  >   5. Floor: Fourth door (`Factory Key`)
