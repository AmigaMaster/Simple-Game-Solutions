# Shards of God - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 06.10.2024 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [itch.io](https://hvavra.itch.io/shards-of-god)

## 1. Mun's Chambers

- EXAMINE emperor
  - EXAMINE wound
  - EXAMINE body
- EXAMINE suit
  - EXAMINE blood
  - EXAMINE metal bar
  - USE metal bar
- EXAMINE plate (right next to the suit)
- EXAMINE empty space (on the right side, there is a plate missing)
- EXAMINE balcony
- TALK TO mother Tiabata, always choose the first answer
- EXIT east

## 2. Library

- EXAMINE decrees
  - EXAMINE **all six** decrees
- EXAMINE lamp right next to the decrees
- EXIT west

## 3. Forge

- EXAMINE tools
- TAKE tools (`Chisel`, `Hammer`, `Screwdriver`)
- EXAMINE machine
  - EXAMINE text
  - USE `Amulet of Vigil` with sensor
  - EXAMINE text
- EXIT west

## 4. Temple

- EXAMINE **all six** carvings, for each:
  - EXAMINE carving
  - EXAMINE inscription
- TALK TO Catalus
  - When asked for your mission: "It's a secret"
- EXIT west

## 5. Farms

- TAKE fruit (`Gnaath fruit`)
- TALK TO Farmer
  - When asked, select: "I have a job for you"
- Walk left
- EXAMINE knife (in the fruit)
- TALK TO Radina
  - When asking for the knife:
    - "I think I can get it unstuck" (`Knife and fruit`)
- EXIT east

## 6. Forge

- USE `Knife and fruit` with furnace (`Knife`, `Fruit glue`)

## 7. Mun's Chambers

- EXAMINE suit
  - USE `Knife` with metal bar

## 8. Library

- TALK TO Xavian
  - "Where can I find the writings of Hepacles?"
- USE `Amulet of Vigil` with vault
- TAKE scroll (`Scroll`)
- EXAMINE `Scroll`
- EXAMINE (right) lamp
  - USE `Screwdriver` with **all three** screws
  - TAKE bulb
  - USE `Gnaath fruit` with holder
- **REPEAT** this for two further lamps
- USE the fourth lamp

### 8.1. Secret Room

- EXAMINE image
- EXAMINE scales
  - USE weighing pan with dais **4 times** (put all weighs on the dais)
  - "The killer stole..."
- EXIT west

## 9. Temple

- TALK TO Catalus
  - "One golden..."
  - "Your historical carvings..."
- USE `Scroll` with Catalus
- USE `Hammer` with statue (`Madman's Gold`)
  - EXAMINE carving
  - EXAMINE inscription

## 10. Forge

- EXAMINE machine
  - USE `Knife` with receptacle
  - USE `Madman's Gold` with receptacle
  - USE receptacle (`Golden knife`)

## 11. Mun's Chambers

- "Because I thought we solve problems"
- "What's the simple..."
- "I understand"
- EXAMINE suit
  - USE `Golden knife` with metal bar
  - USE `Golden knife` with `Fruit glue` (`Knife with glue`)
  - USE `Knife with glue` with metal bar
- TAKE pillows
- "I have absolutely no idea"
- "Rutilus, the general"

## 12. Forge

- EXAMINE Rutilus
  - EXAMINE hand
  - EXAMINE face
  - EXAMINE strange object
  - TAKE strange object

## 13. Library

- "Mun is not a god!..."
- USE lamp
- **NOTE:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
- ENTER secret passage

### 13.1. Secret Room

- EXAMINE scales
  - TAKE ceramic shard and PUT it on the leader OR emperor's rule side.
  - Each decision has a different ending
