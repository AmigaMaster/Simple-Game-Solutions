  - There are 6 cranks and 7 busts

  - You read somewhere about the busts
  - You already have it in your inventory
  - Look at your `Journal`
  - Page 3/5
  - You must rotate each outer bust to look at the center bust
  - Crank 2: **5 times**
  - Crank 3: **7 times**
  - Crank 4: **4 times**
  - Crank 5: **5 times**
  - Crank 6: **2 times**

##### 36.1.7.1. Vessel Chamber

- USE `Gold coin` with vessel **repeat with the seconds gold coin**
- EXAMINE plinth
  - **RIDDLE:** Alphabet
  - Somewhere you read about Greek letters
  - You already have it in your inventory
  - Look in your `Journal`
  - Page 3/5
  - You can find a Greek alphabet at [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_alphabet)
  - Greek alphabet with letters and names:
    ```
    Αα  Alpha
    Ββ  Beta
    Γγ  Gamma
    Δδ  Delta
    Εε  Epsilon
    Ζζ  Zeta
    Ηη  Eta
    Θθ  Theta
    Ιι  Iota
    Κκ  Kappa
    Λλ  Lambda
    Μμ  Mu
    Νν  Nu
    Ξξ  Xi
    Οο  Omicron
    Ππ  Pi
    Ρρ  Rho
    Σσς Sigma
    Ττ  Tau
    Υυ  Upsilon
    Φφ  Phi
    Χχ  Chi
    Ψψ  Psi
    Ωω  Omega
    ```
  - Press `Ρ (Rho), θ (Theta), E (Epsilon), Ξ (Xi)`
  - INTERACT with fragment (`Plinth fragment`)
  - INTERACT with rod (`Coiled rod`)
- INTERACT with wheel (in the left wall)
  - INTERACT with carving (`Ouroboros rod`)
  - **RIDDLE:** Wheel
    - USE `Serpent key` with slot
    - You can rotate an inner and an outer circle
    - Each circle does something in your environment
    - The outer circle moves the left serpent statue in the Serpent Chamber
    - The inner circle moves the right serpent statue
    - You can insert the rods into the holes
    - Specific rods can only be inserted into the left or right holes
    - You need to align the rod position with the arrow position
    - If you do so, a serpent will emit a light ray from its mouth in the Serpent Chamber
    - You need to align the serpents so they lighten the points in the stone in the middle of the Serpent chamber
    - USE `Ouroboros rod` with the middle left hole and align the arrow from the outer circle to the same position
    - USE `Coiled rod` with upper right hole and align he arrow of the inner circle to the same position
