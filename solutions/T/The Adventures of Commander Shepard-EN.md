# The Adventures of Commander Shepard - Walkthrough

| Version | Datum      | Autor                                     |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 06.11.2022 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Get the game for free at [Itch.io](https://kungfucircus.itch.io/the-adventures-of-commander-shepard)

## 1. Normandy Dock: Bay D24

### 1.1. Entrance

- EXIT southeast

### 1.2. Stairs

- TALK TO hologram
- EXIT southeast and travel to Presidium Commons

## 2. Presidium Commons

### 2.1. Entrance

- TALK TO VOLUS Dude
- EXIT north

### 2.2. Meridian Place, Upper Level

- TALK TO Quarian
- TALK TO Salarian
- TALK TO security officer
- EXIT west

### 2.3. Meridian Place, Middle Level

- TALK TO man (`Audio Log`)
- TALK TO clerk (`Space Hamster`)
- Make your way back to the elevator

## 3. Citadel Embassies

### 3.1. Main Entrance Hall

- TALK TO hologram
- EXIT west

### 3.2. Left Entrance Hall

- TALK TO left Krogan
- USE patch on the east side of the screen to try to listen to the conversation
- EXIT south

### 3.3. Main Entrance Hall

- EXIT northeast

### 3.4. Hallway

- TALK TO Elcor
- EXIT northwest

### 3.5. End of Hallway

- TALK TO man
- USE `Audio Log` with man

## 4. Presidium Commons

### 4.1. Meridian Place, Middle Level

- USE `Audio Log` with man

### 4.2. Meridian Place, Upper Level

- USE `Audio Log` with Quarian (`Listening Device`)

## 5. Citadel Embassies

### 5.1. Left  Entrance Hall

- USE `Listening Device` with Krogan
- TALK TO Krogan

## 6. Presidium Commons

### 6.1. Meridian Place, Lower Level

### 6.2. Meridian Place, Middle Level

- TALK TO clerk (`Fish`)
