## 1. Introduction

### 1.1. Executioner's Tower

#### 1.1.1. Bedroom

- EXIT west

#### 1.1.2. Hallway

- EXIT west

#### 1.1.3. Kitchen

- TALK TO Anna
- PICK UP kitchen scraps (`Kitchen Scraps`)
- EXIT east

#### 1.1.4. Hallway

- EXIT south

#### 1.1.5. Backyard

- USE `Kitchen Scraps` on pig
- PICK UP clothes
- EXIT east

#### 1.1.6. Hallway

- EXIT east

#### 1.1.7. Treatment Room

- USE door
- USE storage cupboard **2 times** (`Bottle of Spirits`)
- PICK UP mortal and pestle (from the cupboard) (`Mortar`)
- PICK UP medicinal herbs (from the left window) (`Medicinal Herbs`)
- USE shelf with bandages and cloths (`Cloth`)
- USE `Medicinal Herbs` on `Mortar` (`Ground Herbs`)
- USE `Ground Herbs` on `Bottle of Spirits` (`Tincture`)
- USE `Tincture` on `Cloth` (`Soaked Cloth`)
- USE `Soaked Cloth` on Kaspar

### 1.2. Artist's House

#### 1.2.1. Workshop

- EXAMINE easel with unfinished painting
- EXAMINE silver crow (in the painting)
- PICK up black feather (on the floor to the left of the painting) (`Black Feather from Breitsam's workshop`)
- EXAMINE candle
- EXAMINE wounds
- EXAMINE face
- EXAMINE Leinen sack oder the railing
- TALK TO witch hunter Johann von Kanten

## 2. Chapter 1: Ash and Wax

### 2.1. Town Hall

#### 2.1.1. Council Courtyard

- EXIT north

#### 2.1.2. Dungeon

- USE Dungeon door
- ENTER dungeon

##### 2.1.2.1. Hallway

- USE door to torture chamber
- ENTER torture chamber

###### 2.1.2.1.1. Torture Chamber

- EXAMINE chains
- PICK UP pliers (`Pliers`)
- EXIT west

##### 2.1.2.2. Hallway

- TALK TO Bruno
- ENTER cell

###### 2.1.2.2.1. Cell

- TALK TO Lucas Weinzel
- USE `Pliers` on Lucas Weinzel
- TALK TO Lucas Weinzel **again** (New Location: `Weinzel's House`)
- USE `Map` (or exit east, north, north, west, west, north)

### 2.2. Weinzel's House

- ENTER entrance

#### 2.2.2. Alchemy Laboratory

- EXIT east

- USE drawer **2 times** (`Sealed Letter`)

### 4.15. Notary

- USE `Mended Shawm` on Street Musicians
  - USE all dialogue options (the Musicians will leave)

#### 4.15.1. Study

- TALK TO Notary **2 times**
  - Try some names, then end conversation
- **QUICK:**
  - USE property deeds and contracts (the shelf left to the Notary)
    - "J-L" (`Documents`)
      - **NOTE:** If you are too slow, the Notary will notice you and you have to talk to him again to get him distracted
