# Twisted Times in Twinsmouth - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 30.07.2023 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Get the free game at [https://marcogiorgini.itch.io/twisted-times-in-twinsmouth](https://marcogiorgini.itch.io/twisted-times-in-twinsmouth)

## 1. Path (West)

> Marco

- USE `Invitation` with fishman
- EXIT east

## 2. Downtown

- TALK TO E. A. Poe
- ENTER carpentry

### 2.1. Carpentry

- TALK TO R. L. Styne
  - tell him about Poe request
- TAKE tools (`Awl`)
- EXIT east

## 3. Downtown

- ENTER TrekCon

### 3.1. TrekCon

- TALK TO G. R. R. Martyn
- LOOK AT screenplay
- LOOK AT spyglass
- EXIT east

## 4. Downtown

- ENTER Hotel hall

### 4.1. Hotel Hall

- USE box (`Twinsmouth East map`)
- TALK TO E. A. Poe
- TALK TO H. P. Lovecraft
- USE box **again**
  - **NOTE:** Room name `Ophelia`
- TALK TO H. P. Lovecraft (`Ophelia room key`)
- EXIT west

#### 4.1.1. Corridor

- USE `Ophelia room key` with left door
- ENTER (most left) door

##### 4.1.1.1. Ophelia Room

- TAKE crystal sneakers (`Crystal sneakers`)
- EXIT room and EXIT hotel

## 5. Downtown

- EXIT east

## 6. Path (East)

- TALK TO Emilio Salgari

## 7. Downtown

- EXIT south (at the statue)

## 8. Lighthouse

- TALK TO Jules Verne
- LOOK AT the boat

## 9. Downtown

### 9.1. Hotel Hall

#### 9.1.1. Hotel Lounge

- TALK TO E. A. Poe

### 9.2. Hotel Hall

- USE guest register (`Venice room key`)

#### 9.2.1. Corridor

- USE `Venice room key` with most right back door
- ENTER door

##### 9.2.1.1. Venice Room

- TAKE screenplay (`Screenplay`)
- EXIT hotel

### 9.3. TrekCon

- TAKE spyglass (`Spyglass`)
  - **INFO:** You need to have the `Screenplay` in your inventory to successfully get the spyglass

## 10. Path (East)

- LOOK AT statue
- EXIT east

## 11. Dahn's House

- TALK TO Dhan (`Boat key`)
- TAKE plant (`Curry leaves`)
- TAKE oil (`Curry oil`)

## 12. Lighthouse

- GIVE `Spyglass` to Jules Verne
- USE `Boat key` with boat

## 13. Cave

- LOOK AT panel
- USE panel
- USE boat

## 14. Downtown

- TALK TO G. R. R. Martyn
- EXIT north to the road

## 15. Road

- LOOK AT giant octopus-like thing
- TAKE feather (`White feather`)

## 16. Downtown

### 16.1. Hotel Hall

#### 16.1.1. Hotel Lounge

- TALK TO Emilio Salgari
- TALK TO H. P. Lovecraft
- TALK TO Edgar Allen Poe
- EXIT hotel

### 16.2. Carpentry

- TALK TO R. L. Styne
- TALK TO J. K. Rowlyng

## 17. Path (West)

- TAKE cut branches (`Wand`)
- USE `White feather` with `Wand` (`Powered wand`)

## 18. Downtown

### 18.1. Carpentry

- USE `Powered wand` with puppet

## 19. Road

- TALK TO puppet
- USE `Curry leaves` with puppet
- TALK TO puppet
- ENTER corridor (to the mansion)

### 19.1. Corridor

- EXIT east (it will fail)
- TALK TO E. A. Poe
- TALK TO H.P. Lovecraft

## 20. Downtown

### 20.1. Carpentry

- LOOK AT workbench
- TAKE workbench (`Panel saw`)

## 21. Road

### 21.1. Corridor

- USE `Panel saw` (it will not work)

## 22. Path (East)

- USE `Awl` with statue
- CHANGE TO Stefania

## 23. Path

> Stefania

- TALK TO Dan Brownx
- EXIT west

## 24. Downtown

- ENTER General store

### 24.1. Drugstore

- LOOK AT the books section
- USE fresh (`Sandwich`)
- LOOK AT food section
- TALK TO postal clerk
- TALK TO drugstore clerk
- EXIT west

## 25. Downtown

- ENTER hotel

### 25.1. Hotel

- TALK TO hotel owner
- TAKE hotel business cards (`Hotel business card`)
- LOOK AT cleaning tools
- EXIT west

#### 25.1.1. Room

- LOOK AT Marco's laptop
- EXIT hotel

## 26. Downtown

- EXIT north

## 27. Road

- LOOK AT mansion garden
- EXIT west

### 27.1. Museum

- TALK TO museum employee
- EXIT east

## 28. Road

- EXIT south

## 29. Downtown

- EXIT south

## 30. Lighthouse

- TALK TO Professor Patt
- GIVE `Sandwich` to Professor Patt

## 31. Downtown

- EXIT west

## 32. Railway Station

- USE mapholder (`Pinkhouse Cove map`)
- LOOK AT curry cups
- TALK TO Fish'n'Chips vendor

## 33. Downtown

- LOOK AT hotel

## 34. General Store

- USE food section (`Chili bottle`)
- USE `Chili bottle` with `Sandwich` (`Chili flavored Sandwich`)

## 35. Lighthouse

- GIVE `Chili flavored Sandwich` TO Professor Patt
- TAKE ticket (`Museum ticket`)

## 36. Road

### 36.1. Museum

- GIVE `Museum ticket` to museum employee
- LOOK AT history panel
- LOOK AT **all three** piece of statue
- LOOK AT picture
- LOOK AT keypad
- ENTER storage room door (locked)
- TALK TO museum employee

## 37. Downtown

### 37.1. General Store

- TALK TO postal clerk

## 38. Railway Station

- TALK TO Fish 'n Chips vendor (`Local curry sauce`, `Fish 'n Chips`)

## 39. Downtown

### 39.1. Hotel

#### 39.1.1. Room

- USE `Smartphone`

### 39.2. Hotel

- LOOK AT notebook
- USE notebook
- EXIT west into the room

## 40. Road

### 40.1. Museum

- USE keypad

### 40.2. Mansion Garden

- LOOK AT window
- TALK TO Dan Brownx
- ENTER window

#### 40.2.1. Mansion Library

- LOOK AT books
- USE books (`Lucy's diary`)
- LOOK AT wooden chest (`Old letter`)
- LOOK AT picture
- EXIT west

#### 40.2.2. Mansion Garden

- LOOK AT ground
- TAKE ground (`A human hand`)

## 41. Path

- LOOK AT ugly big plant
- TALK TO Dan Brownx
- USE `Old letter`
- USE `Local curry sauce` with ugly big plant
- USE `A human hand` with ugly big plant
- USE `Lucy's diary` with ugly big plant
- SWITCH to Marco

## 42. Path (West)

> Marco

- TAKE hand (`A human hand`)
- TAKE diary (`Lucy's diary`)

### 44.1. Museum

> Stefania

### 45.1. Hotel

#### 45.1.1. Room

> Marco

## 49. Cave

### 53.2. Luigi's Room
