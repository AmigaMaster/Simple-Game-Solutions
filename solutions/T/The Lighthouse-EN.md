## Lighthouse

- PICK UP matches (from the table) (`Matches`)
- USE `Matches` on stove
- PICK UP wrench (`Wrench`)
- USE `Wrench` on machine gears (in the signal receiver)
- USE paper
- USE telescope
- **ATTENTION:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
- USE cozy chair
- In the dialogue, CHOOSE as you like
- USE telescope
- USE staircase
