#### 1.16.4. Erics Room

- EXAMINE desk
  - **NOTE:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
  - Each box on the engraving grid corresponds to a drawer
  - Each part between the vertical bar line on the music sheet also corresponds to a drawer. Each part between a bar line is called a measure
  - The symbols on the left of each line on the music sheet are clefts. From top to bottom: tenor clef, violin / treble clef, bass clef
  - On the engraving you find the same clefts but with a number
  - This number refers to the individual note on the music sheet (between each bar line). Example: In the first measure of the last row, there are two black and one white notes. In the second measure, there are 5 black notes and in the third measure, there are 2 white and one black notes.
  - The first row in the engraving corresponds to the 3rd note in the 1st measure, the 1st note in the 2nd measure and the 3rd note in the 3rd measure in the treble clef row (black, white, black)
  - You can guess which color corresponds to the drawer status (open or closed)
  - Black = drawer is closed, white = drawer is open
  - Solution to this riddle:
    ```
    Closed Open   Open
    Open   Closed Open
    Closed        Open
    ```
  - EXAMINE secret compartment (`Pop-Pop`)
