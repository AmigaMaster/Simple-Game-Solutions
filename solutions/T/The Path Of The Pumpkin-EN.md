|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------

Download the free game from [Adventure Game Studio](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/play/game/1638-the-path-of-the-pumpkin)

## 1. Arena

- ENTER cell

## 2. Cell

- USE `Vacumpkin` on ? (a small hotspot in the bathtub) (`Full Vacumpkin`)
- LOOK AT `Full Vacumpkin` (`Live Off`)
- EXIT cell

## 3. Arena

- USE `Live Off` on Jack (in the left window)
- USE eject button (the red thing on Bistian's back - you have to move the cursor a little bit right und below the red button) (`Bistian Head`)
- USE `Bistian Head` on balcony (where Duke Dice is standing)

- TALK TO Kika (`Love Sweater`)
- USE door (to the yellow building)

- USE Feefee's stuff (`Painting Kit`)
- EXIT upstairs

#### 4.1.1. Upper Level

- PICK UP broom (`Broom`)
- TALK TO Pablo Kockatiel
- EXIT downstairs

### 4.2. Office
