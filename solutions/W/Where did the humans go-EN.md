# Where did the humans go - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 21.10.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [itch.io](https://lorenzob.itch.io/where-did-the-humans-go)

## 1. Landing Site

- PICK UP navigational robot (`Navigational Robot`)
- PICK UP emergency box (`Emergency Box`)
- LOOK AT `Emergency Box` (`Food Rations`, `Universal Screwdriver`)
- USE ship controls (in the cockpit)
- USE open panel (`List of objects needed`)
- EXIT south

## 2. Gate

- TALK TO gate guardian
  - "Yes."
- EXIT north

## 3. Camp

### 3.1. Courtyard

- ENTER tailor on the right

#### 3.1.1. Tailor

- TALK TO weird tailor bot
- PICK UP wet rag (`Wet Rag`)
- EXIT southwest

### 3.2. Courtyard

- ENTER house

#### 3.2.1. House

- PICK UP battery charger (`Battery Charger`)
- PICK UP fashion magazine (`Fashion Magazine`)
- PICK UP Eiffel Tower souvenir (`Eiffel Tower Souvenir`)
- PICK UP plastic strap (on the cupboard behind the exercise bike) (`Elastic stretching Strap`)
- EXIT west

### 3.3. Courtyard

- ENTER kitchen

#### 3.3.1. Kitchen

- PICK UP strainer (`Metal Strainer`)
- PICK UP weird tray (`Weird Baking Tray`)
- PICK UP baking soda (`Baking Soda`)
- TALK TO robotic cook
- USE `Food Rations` on robotic cook
- PICK UP bottles of motor oil (`Bottle of Motor Oil`)
- EXIT west

### 3.4. Courtyard

- ENTER infirmary

#### 3.4.1. Infirmary

##### 3.4.1.1. Entrance Hall

- USE `Screwdriver` on glass window (`Piece of Glass`)
- EXIT west

##### 3.4.1.2. Surgery Room

- USE `Wet Rag` on `Baking Soda`
- USE `Wet Rag` on car battery
- USE `Battery Charger` on car battery (`Welder`)

#### 3.4.2. Tailor

- USE `Elastic stretching Strap` on `Weird Baking Tray` (`Baking Tray with a Strap`)
- USE `Piece of Glass` on `Baking Tray with a Strap` (`Makeshift Welder Mask`)
- USE `Screwdriver` on `Metal Strainer` (`Metal Strainer without T`)
- USE USE `Eiffel Tower Souvenir` on `Metal Strainer without T` (`Makeshift Helmet`)
- USE `Makeshift Helmet` on weird tailor bot (`Flight Control Stick`, `Keycard`)

#### 3.4.3. Infirmary

##### 3.4.3.1. Entrance Hall

- EXIT east

##### 3.4.3.2. Nursery

- USE `Navigational Robot` on robotic nurse
- TALK TO robotic nurse
- **QUICK:**
  - PICK UP jerry can (`Jerry Can`)

### 3.5. Courtyard

- EXIT north

### 3.6. Tower

- USE `Keycard` on keycard reader
