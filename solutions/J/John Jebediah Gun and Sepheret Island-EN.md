# John Jebediah Gun And Sepheret Island - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 31.01.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/jjgun-and-sepheret-island)

## 1. Street

- EXIT north into the inn

### 1.1. Adbul’s Inn

- TALK TO barkeeper (`Pass`)
- EXIT west

## 2. Street

- EXIT east (behind the car)

## 3. Tourist Village

### 3.1. Street

- EXIT north

### 3.2. Hotel

#### 3.2.1. Lobby

- LOOK AT television
- TALK TO receptionist
- LOOK AT notice sheets (above the person in the middle)
- USE notice sheets (`Pin`)
- EXIT north (right of the notices)

#### 3.2.2. Bar

- TALK TO Alfredo
- EXIT east

#### 3.2.3. Lobby

- EXIT northeast

#### 3.2.4. Hallway

- LOOK AT dead body
- LOOK AT **all three** evidences
- TALK TO inspector
- EXIT northeast

#### 3.2.5. Party Room

- TALK TO woman
- USE `Pin` on red balloon
- EXIT west

#### 3.2.6. Hallway

- PICK UP ring (`Ring`)

#### 3.2.7. Party Room

- USE `Ring` on Nina
- EXIT west **2 times**, north

#### 3.2.8. Bar

- TALK TO Alfredo
  - "Nina Rogneux accepted..." (`Key`)

#### 3.2.9. Party Room

- EXIT east

### 3.3. Beach

- EXIT north

### 3.4. Pier

- USE `Key` on boat

## 4. Island

### 4.1. Beach

- EXIT west

### 4.2. Outside House

- EXIT west

### 4.3. Forest Crossing

- WALK west until you see a statue
- EXIT south

### 4.4. Village Entrance

- EXIT north

#### 4.4.1. Village Center

- EXIT west

#### 4.4.2. Outside Huts

- ENTER left hut

##### 4.4.2.1. Shaman Hut

- TALK TO shaman
- EXIT east **3 times**

#### 4.4.3. Totem Pole

- PICK UP rope (`Rope with Hook`)
- EXIT west, south **2 times**

### 4.5. Forest Crossing

- WALK to the paths on the right
- EXIT south

### 4.6. Bridge

- EXIT east

### 4.7. Abandon Village

#### 4.7.1. Entrance

- EXIT southeast

#### 4.7.2. Ruin

- PICK UP Uber Cola (`Uber Cola`)
- EXIT west

#### 4.7.3. Entrance

- ENTER pathway into the building

### 4.8. Pathway

- EXIT south

### 4.9. Temple

- ENTER temple on the east

#### 4.9.1. Temple Entrance

- EXIT north

#### 4.9.2. Temple Maze

- **NOTE:** If you make any mistake, you restart from the entrance to the maze
  1. EXIT north
  2. EXIT east
  3. EXIT north

#### 4.9.3. Sculpture Room

- USE `Uber Cola` on robot
- PICK UP robot (`Broken Robot`)
- EXIT west, then east to exit the maze, south, west, north, northeast, west

### 4.10. Forest Crossing

- EXIT north

### 4.11. Pyramids

- USE `Rope with Hook` on hole in the ground (in the middle of the screen)
- USE hole

#### 4.11.1. Cave

##### 4.11.1.1. Entrance

- EXIT north

##### 4.11.1.2. Chamber

- PICK UP piece of a puzzle (in the middle) (`Puzzle Piece`)
- EXIT west

##### 4.11.1.3. Entrance

- USE rope

### 4.12. Pyramids

- ENTER pyramid on the west side

#### 4.12.1. Western Pyramid

- USE panel
  - USE bottom button
  - USE middle button
  - USE top button
- EXIT east

### 4.13. Pyramids

- ENTER pyramid on the east side

#### 4.13.1. Eastern Pyramid

- PICK UP puzzle piece (`Puzzle Piece`)
- EXIT west

### 4.14. Pyramids

- EXIT northeast (return to the forest crossing), east

### 4.15. Outside House

- ENTER house

#### 4.15.1. House

- USE `Uber Cola` on puzzle piece
- PICK UP puzzle piece (`Puzzle Piece`)
- EXIT south, west, southwest, north, west, enter hut

### 4.16. Village Entrance

#### 4.16.1. Outside Huts

##### 4.16.1.1. Shaman Hut

- USE `Broken Robot` on shaman (`Puzzle Piece`)
- EXIT east **2 times**, south **2 times**, southeast, east, northeast, south, east, north, through the maze, north, east, north

### 4.17. Temple

#### 4.17.1. Sculpture Room

- USE **all four** (`Puzzle Piece`) on calendar
- WALK onto the ground relief
- EXIT northeast

#### 4.17.2. Portal Room

- ENTER portal

#### 4.17.3. Tomb Chamber

- PICK UP statue (`Statue`)
- ENTER portal and make your way back to the Beach

#### 4.17.4. Portal Room

- EXIT south, west, east, south, west, north, northeast, west, east **2 times**
