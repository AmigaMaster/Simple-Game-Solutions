# Black Sect Remake - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 17.11.2024 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [Archive.org](https://archive.org/details/black-sect-remake)

## 1. Village of Issegeac

- OPEN Shutters
- TAKE (left) bar (`bar`)
- USE inside (`cheese`)
- EXIT east

## 2. Hanged Man

- TAKE coin (left to fox) (`coin`)
- EXIT west

## 3. Village of Issegeac

- EXIT north

## 4. Bridge of Silence

- TAKE ? from below tree (`soap`)
- EXIT east

## 5. Cemetery

- USE left eye socket (in skull) (`ring`)
- EXIT west

## 6. Bridge of Silence

- EXIT north

## 7. Northern Passage

- USE `ring` with farmer
- EXIT north

## 8. Serpent's vault

- TAKE knife (`knife`)
- EXIT north

## 9. Ancient Ruins

- TALK TO tramp
- USE `cheese` with tramp
- EXIT south **4 times**

## 10. Village of Issegeac

- OPEN (right) door (to the inn)
- ENTER tavern

### 10.1. Tavern

- TALK TO innkeeper
- USE `coin` with innkeeper (`bread`)
- EXIT tavern

## 11. Village of Issegeac

- TALK TO musician
- EXIT east

## 12. Hanged Man

- USE `knife` with rope
- EXIT west, then north **4 times**

## 13. Ancient Ruins

- USE 3rd stone from wall where tramp was sitting
- USE 4th stone from wall where tramp was sitting
- USE `bar` with 4th stone
- LOOK AT hole (`glove`)
- USE `glove` with bush (`small bottle`)
- EXIT north

## 14. Accursed Chapel

- EXIT east

## 15. Devil's Well

- TAKE pitchfork (`pitchfork`)
- EXIT south, west

## 16. Old Cross

- TAKE coin (`coin`)
- USE base of the cross (`small key`)
- EXIT east, south **3 times**, west

## 17. Barren Lands

- USE (trunk's) opening
- USE `pitchfork` with (trink's) opening (`sieve`)
- USE `sieve` with river (just below the tree in the background) (`gold nugget`)
- EXIT south **2 times**

## 18. Bridge of Silence

- EXIT south and return
- USE `soap` with washer (`pin`)
- EXIT north

## 19. Northern Passage

- Walk around in all directions and keep getting back here until the farmer appears
- USE `knife` with farmer
- USE `gold nugget` with farmer
- USE `knife` with farmer
- EXIT south **2 times**, east

## 20. Hanged Man

- USE `knife`with rope
- USE `glove` with dwarf (`score`)
- EXIT west

## 21. Village of Issegeac

- USE `score` with musician (`page from a missal`)
- LOOK AT `page from a missal`
- EXIT north **4 times**

## 22. Ancient Ruins

- Walk around until tramp appears
- USE `bread` with tramp (`tablet`)
- LOOK AT `tablet`
  - **NOTE:** The text and numbers `Sky 2 1 3 8 Earth`
- EXIT north

## 23. Accursed Chapel

- TALK TO priest
- EXIT south **5 times**

## 24. Village of Issegeac

### 24.1. Tavern

- USE `coin` with innkeeper
- USE `small bottle` with barrel (`bottle with wine`)
- EXIT tavern, north **5 times**

## 25. Accursed Chapel

- USE `bottle with wine` with priest (`chapel key`)
- USE `chapel key` with door
- ENTER door

### 25.1. Inside the Chapel

- TAKE missal (`missal`)
- USE left arm (of Statue)
- EXIT south, east

## 26. Devil's Well

- DOWN the well

### 26.1. Bottom of the Well

- LOOK AT `tablet`
  - **NOTE:** 4 numbers `2 1 3 8`
  - USE 2nd bar from top
  - USE 1st bar from top
  - USE 3th bar from top
  - USE 8th bar from top
- USE open compartment (`key (golden)`)
- EXIT east

### 26.2. Echo Chamber

- USE `key (golden)` with door
- EXIT east

### 26.3. An Old Store Room

- TAKE rope
- USE `pitchfork` with straw (`piece of paper`)
- USE (right) horseshoe
- USE `pin` with small hole (behind the horseshoe)
- TAKE crucifix (`crucifix`)
- EXIT south **2 times**, up, south

## 27. Accursed Chapel

- ENTER door

### 27.1. Inside the Chapel

- USE `crucifix` with small cross
- USE small cross
- EXIT down

### 27.2. Secret Sacristy

- USE `small key` with door
- TAKE cassock
- LOOK AT under the closet
  - LOOK AT `missal`
    - **NOTE:** the numbers on the right side `1 3 3 2 1`
  - USE buttons in the order from the missal page
  - USE inside (`pentacle`)
- EXIT south

### 27.3. Inside the Chapel

- EXIT south **5 times**, east

## 28. Cemetery

- LOOK AT `pentacle`
  - CHANGE letters to `MOLOCH`
- USE `pentacle` with mouth (of the skull)

## 29. Hall Of Shadows

- OPEN door
- EXIT east

## 30. Hall Of Sentinel

- EXIT east

## 31. Dining Hall

- TALK TO guard of the dining hall
- EXIT east

## 32. Quiet Hall

- OPEN trunk
- USE inside (`rope`)
- USE shield
- USE not so secret compartment (`paper`)
- USE candle holder
- USE drawer (`brooch`)
- EXIT east

## 33. Draped Hall

- EXIT east

## 34. Mysterious Gate

- USE armor
- USE bottom half (of armor) (`key`)
- EXIT west **2 times**

## 35. Quiet Hall

- USE `key` with door
- ENTER door

### 35.1. Prison

- TAKE dish (`dish`)
- USE straw (`lighter`)
- USE (middle vertical) bar
- USE compartment (`letter from prisoner`)
- LOOK AT `letter from prisoner`
- EXIT south, east

## 36. Draped Hall

- USE `lighter` with fireplace
- TAKE ring (under table) (`ring (with cross)`)
- USE curtain
- TAKE key (`key (draped hall)`)
- USE candle
- USE candle holder (`empty scroll`)
- USE loose tile (in front of table)
- USE hole `ring (with black stone)`
- EXIT west **3 times**

## 37. Hall Of Sentinel

- WAIT until guard leaves
- USE `ring (with cross)` with shield
- EXIT west

## 38. Hall Of Shadows

- USE `key (draped hall)` with lock
- USE inside (`dagger`)
- EXIT west

## 39. Basement

- USE `rope` with pulley
- USE rope
- TAKE shield (`shield`)
- LOOK AT carvings at the wooden beam at the top
- LOOK AT `paper`
  - **NOTE:** `X, square, circle, square, triangle`
- USE hams in order of this sequence
- USE (lower left ) crack (in the left pillar) (`woodworm`)
- EXIT south, east

## 40. Hall Of Sentinel

- TAKE foxglove (on wall) (`foxglove`)
- EXIT north

## 41. Library

- LOOK AT book (on the right table)
- USE green book (book of Astharoth, mentioned in prisoner's letter) on bottom line of shelf
- TAKE piece of paper (`recipe`)
- LOOK AT `recipe`
- EXIT south, east

## 42. Dining Hall

- USE `brooch` with guard of the dining hall

### 42.1. Kitchen

- USE `ring (with black stone)` with cook (`medallion`)
- USE `recipe` with cook
- USE `foxglove` with cook
- USE `woodworm` with cook
- USE cupboard
- USE inside (`chicken claw`)
- USE `chicken claw` with cook
- EXIT south, east **3 times**

## 43. Mysterious Gate

- USE `medallion`with disciple
- EXIT north

## 44. Northern Corridor

- EXIT west

## 45. Master's Chamber

- TAKE sword (`sword`)
- USE `dagger`with pillow
- USE pillow (`small bottle`)
- USE `small bottle` with `empty scroll` (`scroll with message`)
- LOOK AT `scroll with message`
  - **NOTE:** `CLINE`
- USE `shield` with shape on wall (above the chair)
- USE under the bed (`key (from under bed)`)
- TAKE gem (`gem`)
- TAKE pendant (`pendant`)
- EXIT east, south

## 46. Mysterious Gate

- USE top half (to reassemble armor)
- USE `sword` with armor
- EXIT west **6 times**

## 47. Basement

- LOOK AT 1st button (at the table)
  - **NOTE:** `S N I`
- LOOK AT 4th button (at the table)
  - **NOTE:** `E L C`
- ENTER `CLINE`
  - 6th button
  - 5th button
  - 3rd button
  - 2nd button
  - 4th button
- USE drawer (`powder box`)
- EXIT south, east **2 times**

## 48. Dining Hall

- USE `gem` with guard of the dining hall

### 48.1. Kitchen

- USE `powder box` with cook
- USE `dish` with cook (`meal`)
- EXIT south, east **3 times**, north
