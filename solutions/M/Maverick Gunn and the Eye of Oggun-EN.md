# Maverick Gunn and the Eye of Oggun - Walkthrough

|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 22.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

## 1. Destiny Island

### 1.1. Maverick's Office

#### 1.1.1. Living Room

- EXIT east

#### 1.1.2. Bedroom

- OPEN wardrobe
- TAKE baseball cap (`Baseball Cap`)
- TAKE sunglasses (from the shelf under the small window) (`Sunglasses`)
- EXIT west

#### 1.1.3. Living Room

- OPEN door
- EXIt through door

### 1.2. Library

#### 1.2.1. Yard

- TALK TO gardener (Silas)
- ENTER doorway

#### 1.2.2. Library

- TALK TO Ms. Garret
- EXIT Library

### 1.3. Travel Agency

- TALK TO Paddy

### 1.4. Library

#### 1.4.1. Yard

- TALK TO gardener
  - "Paddy says you told him about a book and monks."

#### 1.4.2. Library

- TALK TO Ms. Garret
  - "I'm looking for a book written by Perales."
- USE books

### 1.5. Ruins

- TALK TO archeologist
  - "Are you familiar with Jaques de Sores?"
  - "About that secret signature."
- LOOK AT `Old Paper`
- TALK TO archeologist
  - "Are you say Jaques de Sores was here?"
  - "What's in your head?"
  - "Wow! You set camp in the wrong place?"
  - "So, tell me the full story."
  - "You are in a problem then."
  - "About your proposition." (`(Red) Stone Disc`)

### 1.6. Hilton Palace

#### 1.6.1. Lobby

- TALK TO bodyguard
  - "I need to see Mr. Mbeka."
  - "I really have to see your boss."
  - "I have you a proposition."
- USE elevator

### 1.7. Lighthouse

#### 1.7.1. Entrance

- OPEN door

#### 1.7.2. Top of Lighthouse

- TALK TO Barnaby
  - "I'm working in a case."
  - "What do you think of my employer?"
- EXIT downstairs

### 1.8. Maverick's Office

#### 1.8.1. Living Room

- TAKE sun protection lotion (`Sunscreen Bottle Empty`)

### 1.9. Travel Agency

- TALK TO Paddy
  - "Say Paddy, what's with that bottle on the desk?" (`Rum`)
  - "I'd like to use one of your charters."
  - "I'd like to go to Guanava Island." (`Ticket`)

### 1.10. Hilton Palace

#### 1.10.1. Lobby

- USE `Sunscreen Bottle Empty` with sunscreen bottle (`Sunscreen Bottle Full`)
- GIVE `Rum` to bodyguard

### 1.11. Docks

- **NOTE:** If you at any point have problem exiting the scenes and you character will stop at the screen borders, try pressing the corresponding cursor keys to successfully exit the scene
- TALK TO Captain Shane
- GIVE `Ticket` to Captain Shane

## 2. Guanava Island

### 2.1. Jungle Docks

- TALK TO men in blue
- GIVE `Baseball Cap` to men in blue
- GIVE `Sunglasses` to men in blue
- GIVE `Sunscreen Bottle Full` to men in blue
- EXIT east

### 2.2. Jungle Path with Quicksand

- EXIT east

### 2.3. Village

- TALK TO music lover
- TALK TO woman
  - "Is that stone disc yours?"
- TALK TO chief (`Beer`)
- EXIT east

### 2.4. Jungle Path with Boulder

- TAKE clay pot (`Clay Pot`)
- EXIT east

### 2.5. River

- EXIT west

### 2.6. Village

- TALK TO woman
  - "Still no change in the tribe?"
- USE `Clay Pot` with cauldron (`Clay Pot Full`)
- TALK TO chief
  - "Any landmarks I should see?"

### 2.7. Jungle Docks

- USE `Clay Pot Full` with boards
- TAKE boards (`Boards`)

### 2.8. River

- USE `Boards` with spot for a bridge
- TAKE fruit (at the northern side of the river to the east) (`Fruit`)
- EXIT north to the path into the woods

### 2.9. Jungle Path to Cave

- TALK TO guardian
- EXIT east

### 2.10. Cave Entrance

- PULL branch (`Forked Branch`)

### 2.11. Jungle Path with Quicksand

- USE `Forked Branch` with machete (`Machete`)

### 2.12. Jungle Docks

- GIVE `Ticket` to Captain Shane
- TALK TO Captain Shane
  - "Can we go back?"
  - "About the beer."
  - "I have a bottle of cold beer."
- GIVE `Ticket` to Captain Shane

## 3. Destiny Island

### 3.1. Lighthouse

#### 3.1.1. Top of Lighthouse

- TALK TO Barnaby
  - "Can I borrow a can of tuna from the pile?"
- GIVE `Fruit` to Barnaby

### 3.2. Library

#### 3.2.1. Library

- LOOK AT Sudoku (on the table)
- TAKE Sudoku (`Sudoku Book`)

### 3.3. Lighthouse

#### 3.3.1. Top of Lighthouse

- GIVE `Sudoku Book` to Barnaby
- TALK TO Barnaby
  - "Can I take the can of tuna now?" (`Tuna`)

## 4. Guanava Island

### 4.1. Village

- GIVE `Tuna` to woman (`(Green) Stone Disc`)

### 4.2. Cave Entrance

- ENTER tunnel

### 4.3. Pyramid

- USE `(Red) Stone Disc` with hole
- PUSH red stone disc
- USE `Machete` with thorny bush
- USE `(Green) Stone Disc` with hole
- PUSH green stone disc
- ENTER entrance (`Gold Idol`)
- EXIT east

#### 4.3.1. Tunnel

- EXIT west
- EXIT north

### 4.4. Pyramid

- EXIT to the (right) side of pyramid
- EXIT to the (left) side of pyramid

### 4.5. Jungle Docks

- GIVE `Ticket` to Captain Shane

## 5. Destiny Island

### 5.1. Ruins

- GIVE `Gold Idol` to archeologist
  - LOOK AT **all four** stones
  - TAKE chisel (on the right side) (`Chisel`)
  - USE `Chisel` with center stone
  - TAKE treasure (`Eye of Oggun`, `Treasure`)

### 5.2. Hilton Palace

- TALK TO bodyguard
  - "I have to see your boss again."
