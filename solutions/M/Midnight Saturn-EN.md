# Midnight Saturn - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 28.09.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

## 1. Main Menu

- >ACHIEVEMENT: Credits
  >- Watch credits

## 2. Arcade

- PICK UP Paper (`Spoiled Cheat Sheet`)
- PICK UP Piggy Bank (`Piggy Bank`)
- TALK TO Clive
- USE Cable
- TALK TO Donny
- TALK TO Boone (New Location: Fair)
- TALK TO Michelle
- USE Phone

## 3. Fair

- LOOK AT Vendor Booth (`Plush Toy`)
- PICK UP Card (`Coffee Loyalty Card`)
- TALK TO Phoebe (New Location: Coffee Cove)
- USE `Piggy Bank` with Crabby (`Pill Vial`)
- LOOK AT grass (below Crabby)

## 4. Coffee Cove

- PICK UP Pastries (`Cupcake`, `Cinnamon Oil`)
- TALK TO Zoey
- USE `Coffee Loyalty Card` with Zoey (`Super Coffee`, `Ice`)
- USE `Pill Vial` with `Super Coffee` (`Spiked Coffee`)
- USE `Ice` with `Spoiled Cheat Sheet` (`Cheat Sheet`)
- TALK TO Dawn
- TALK TO Tarra

## 5. Arcade

- USE `Cheat Sheet` with Donny (New Location: Granger Lane)

## 6. Granger Lane

- LOOK AT Trash Bin (`Brown Tinsel Wig`, `Toothpaste`)
- TALK TO Smoke (New Location: Zora)

## 7. Zora

- TALK TO Zora
- PICK UP soap **2 times** (`Soap`)
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Offering
  >- USE `Cupcake` with Zora

## 8. Granger Lane

- USE `Spiked Coffee` with Smoke (New Location: Cliff)

## 9. Cliff

- USE `Cinnamon Oil` with Rock
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Gemini Butterfly
  >- USE Butterfly (`Pepper`)

## 10. Granger Lane

- TALK TO Smoke (New Location: Lakeside, `Rubber Duck`)

## 11. Lakeside

- USE `Plush Toy` on Bubbles
- USE `Toothpaste` with Crocodile (`Space Helmet`)
- USE `Cupcake` on More Bubbles
- USE `Pepper` with Another Crocodile (`Brooch`)
- USE `Rubber Duck` on Even More Bubbles
- USE `Soap` with Yet Another Crocodile (`Ruby Ring`)

## 12. Zora

- USE `Brown Tinsel Wig` with Pigmented Ethanol (`Pink Tinsel Wig`)
- USE `Brooch` with Zora
- USE `Space Helmet` with Zora
- USE `Ruby Ring` with Zora (New Location: Rushlight Tower)

## 13. Rushlight Tower

- PICK UP Oil Can (`Empty Oil Can`)
- USE `Pink Tinsel Wig`
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Cyan
  >- TALK TO Cyan **4 times**
- WALK east
- USE Satellite Dish
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Satellite
  >- **NOTE:** The riddle
  >- From the sky (`Blue`) to the waiting earth (`Brown`) the sparrow fell. The bird of prey, in pursuit, searched amid the lilac (`Purple`), a fire (`Red`) raged, rolling downhill. And the grass (`Green`) trashed wildly, down towards the burbling brook (`Blue`)
  >- ENTER the code:
  >   1. Blue
  >   2. Brown
  >   3. Purple
  >   4. Red
  >   5. Green
  >   6. Blue (New Location: Forest)
- WALK east
- USE Box (`Duct Tape`, `Pliers`)

## 14. Forest

- LOOK AT Log (`Controller`, `Magnet`)

## 15. Coffee Cove

- USE `Controller` with Dawn (`Turkey Leg from Outer Space`)
- USE `Empty Oil Can` with Tarra (`Rancid Milk`)

## 16. Fair

- USE `Magnet` with Grass (`Toy Soldier`)
- USE `Toy Soldier` with Phoebe (`Xylophone Mallet`)

## 17. Lakeside

- USE `Turkey Leg from Outer Space` with Still More Bubbles
- USE `Rancid Milk` with Still Another Crocodile (`Shovel`, `Smelly Chew Toy`)

## 18. Forest

- WAIT until you hear something, then:
  - HIDE behind the tree on the right
- USE `Shovel` with Disturbed Soil
- ENTER Cave Entrance

### 18.1. Cave Entrance

- PICK UP Gloves (`Insulated Gloves`)
- LOOK AT Pictograph
  - **NOTE:** `1x Frog, 1x Duck, 1x Bat, 2x Duck, 2x Bat, 2x Frog`
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Crystal
  >- USE `Xylophone Mallet` on:
  >   1. Small Crystal (Frog)
  >   2. Fore Crystal (Duck)
  >   3. Far Crystal (Bat)
  >   4. Fore Crystal **2 times**
  >   5. Far Crystal **2 times**
  >   6. Small Crystal **2 times**
- EXIT east

### 18.2. Inner Cave

- USE `Insulating Gloves` with `Duct Tape` (`Insulating Gloves and Duct Tape`)
- USE `Insulating Gloves and Duct Tape` with cables
- >ACHIEVEMENT: Recording
  >- USE Machine

## 19. Fair

- TALK TO Lucy (New Location: Smoke's Parents Home)

## 20. Smoke's Parents Home

- PICK UP Hair Dryer (`Hair Dryer`)
- USE `Smelly Chew Toy` with Microwave (`Disinfected Chew Toy`)
- USE `Disinfected Chew Toy` with Dog (`Tooth`)

## 21. Zora

- USE `Tooth` with Zora

## 22. Fair

- TALK TO Lucy (New Location: Candy Factory)

## 23. Candy Factory

### 23.1. Entrance

- >ACHIEVEMENT: Factory
- USE (right) Panel
- TALK TO Lucy (`Apply Vinegar Hair Spray`)
- USE `Apply Vinegar Hair Spray` with (right) Panel
- USE (right) Panel
- USE `Pliers` with (right) Panel
- USE (left) Panel
- USE `Hair Dryer` with (left) Panel
- USE Control Panel **3 times**
- EXIT east

### 23.2. Deeper Inside

- >ACHIEVEMENT: Aurelian
  >- USE Console
  >   - **NOTE:** The hint: Count from small to large but not by height or weight but by ink and breath
  >   - Sort them by the number of letters in the word
  >     1. Pig
  >     2. Frog
  >     3. Whale
  >     4. Donkey
  >     5. Panther
  >     6. Elephant
  >     7. Crocodile
- TALK TO Aurelian

## 24. Forest

### 24.1. Inner Cave

- TALK TO Lucy (`Hairbrush`)

## 25. Cliff

- USE `Hairbrush` with Butterfly (`Hairbrush with pollen`)

## 26. Candy Factory

### 26.1. Deeper Inside

- USE `Hairbrush with pollen` with Aurelian (`Glass Jar`)

## 27. Candy Factory

- USE `Glass Jar` with Dispenser (`Slime`)

## 28. Smoke's Parents House

- USE `Slime` with Strongbox

## 29. Zora

- >ACHIEVEMENT: Confrontation
  >- TALK TO Zora
