# Pirate Theme Park: A Short Adventure - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 12.12.2023 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Buy the game from [itch.io](https://focushillgames.itch.io/pirate-theme-park)

## 1. Ghost Train Ride

### 1.1. Crossway

- EXIT west

### 1.2. Ghost Train

- LOOK AT crack in the wall
- EXIT north

#### 1.2.1. Entrance

- TALK TO maintenance man

### 1.3. Crossway

- EXIT north

## 2. Darkest Part of the Island

### 2.1. Cave Entrance

- ENTER dark cave

#### 2.1.1. Dark Cave

- TALK TO sorcerer
- EXIT cave

## 3. Maintenance Shed

- PICK UP camping light (`camping light`)
- EXIT west

## 4. Restaurant

### 4.1. Restaurant Entrance

- TALK TO person dressed as a cup
- ENTER restaurant

#### 4.1.1. Restaurant

- PICK UP orange juice (`orange juice`)
- TALK TO waitress (you need some money)
- EXIT restaurant

## 5. Pirate Ship

- TALK TO pirate
- GIVE `orange juice` to pirate (`fridge magnet`)
- EXIT south

## 6. Gift Shop

### 6.1. Entrance

- LOOK AT crate
- PICK UP crate (`bulb`)
- ENTER gift shop

#### 6.1.1. Gift Shop

- TALK TO gift salesman
  - Ask for free gift **3 times** (`bear toy`)
- OPEN `bear toy` (`battery`, `turned off bear toy`)
- EXIT shop

## 7. Beach

- TALK TO ice cream seller
- EXIT beach

## 8. Rollercoaster

- PICK UP tickets from the counter (`rollercoaster ticket`)
- TALK TO clown
- EXIT east

## 9. Small Island

- USE `fridge magnet` with sand (`clown award`)

## 10. Ghost Train Ride

### 10.1. Ghost Train

- USE `fridge magnet` with crack in the wall (`coin`)

## 11. Rollercoaster

- GIVE `clown award` to clown (`meal voucher`)

## 12. Restaurant

### 12.1. Restaurant Entrance

#### 12.1.1. Restaurant

- GIVE `meal voucher` to waitress (`fast food bag`)
- OPEN `fast food bag` (`burger`, `fancy key ring`)

## 13. Pirate Ship

- GIVE `burger` to pirate
- ENTER pirate ship

### 13.1. Upper Deck of the Pirate Ship

- ENTER cabin

#### 13.1.1. Cabin

- PICK UP bug spray (`bug spray`)
- OPEN desk drawer from the desk to thr right (`hand fan`)
- USE `battery` with `hand fan` (`hand fan with power`)
- EXIT ship

## 14. Beach

- GIVE `rollercoaster ticket` to ice cream seller (`ice cream`)

## 15. Vending Machine

- USE `coin` with vending machine
- PUSH vending machine (`magic pretzels`)

## 16. Ghost Train Ride

### 16.1. Ghost Train

#### 16.1.1. Entrance

- USE `battery` with `camping light` (`camping light with bulb`)
- GIVE `camping light with bulb` to maintenance man
- GIVE `bug spray` to maintenance man (`wrench`)

## 17. Restaurant

### 17.1. Restaurant Entrance

- GIVE `ice cream` to person dressed as a cup
- GIVE `hand fan with power` to person dressed as a cup (`gift voucher`)

## 18. Gift Shop

### 18.1. Entrance

#### 18.1.1. Gift Shop

- GIVE `gift voucher` to gift salesman (`diamond ring`)

## 19. Darkest Part of the Island

### 19.1. Cave Entrance

#### 19.1.1. Dark Cave

- GIVE `fancy key ring` to sorcerer
- GIVE `magic pretzels` to sorcerer
- GIVE `wrench` to sorcerer
- GIVE `diamond ring` to sorcerer (`key`)

## 20. Maintenance Shed

- USE `key` with impenetrable metal door

### 20.1. Shed

- ENTER portal
