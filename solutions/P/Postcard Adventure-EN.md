# Postcard Adventure - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 23.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [Adventure Game Studio](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/play/game/2577-postcard-adventure)

## 1. Mountain Passage & Outside Wizard's House

- PICK UP flint and steel (in the tree) (`Flint and Steel`)
- TALK TO General **3 times** (`Pipe`)
- EXIT left

## 2. Golden Door & Cave

- EXIT left

## 3. Dragoncave & Beach

- TALK TO Goblin **2 times**
- TALK TO Dragon **2 times**
- EXIT left

## 4. Cave & Inside Wizard's House

- TALK TO Walder **2 times**
- TALK TO hunter
- EXIT left

## 5. Underwater & Swamp

- TALK TO open window **3 times**
- TALK TO crab
- TALK TO bouncer **2 times**
- PICK UP whetstone (`Whetstone`)
- EXIT right

## 6. Underwater & Swamp

- USE `Thorny Fruits` on basket (`Fish`)
- EXIT left

## 7. Mermaid & Balder

- TALK TO Balder **2 times**
- TALK TO Mermaid
- EXIT right **4 times**

## 8. Golden Door & Cave

- USE plant (`Thorny Fruits`)
- EXIT right **2 times**

## 9. Pink Elephant & Druids

- TALK TO Druid
- EXIT right

## 10. Old Woman & Beach

- TALK TO old woman
- PICK UP salt (`Salt`)
- USE `Fish` on seagull (`Fish Scales`)
- EXIT right

## 11. House & Bridge

- TALK TO Druid
- PICK UP scissors (on the left pillar of the bridge) (`Blunt Scissors`)
- USE `Whetstone` on `Blunt Scissors` (`Sharp Scissors`)
- PICK UP bottle (in the left postcard at t he bottom right corner) (`Medicine`)
- PICK UP red berries (`Berries`)
- TALK TO angry man (`Tobacco Leaves`)
- EXIT right

## 12. Stump & Crystals

- TALK TO Angus the leprechaun
- EXIT right

## 13. Cauldron & Mountain Top

- PICK UP mug (`Mug`)
- TALK TO Druid
- EXIT right

## 14. Two Beaches

- TALK TO lazy man
- PICK UP coconut (`Coconut`)
- USE `Coconut` on lazy man (`Firebrand Leaves`)
- EXIT left **3 times**

## 15. Stump & Crystals

- USE `Mug` on pond (`Clean Water`)

## 16. House & Bridge

- TALK TO Druid

## 17. Pink Elephant & Druids

- USE `Firebrand Leaves` on Druid

## 18. Dragoncave & Beach

- TALK TO Goblin (`Goblin's Hair`)

## 19. Cave & Inside Wizard's House

- TALK TO hunter (`Fur`)

## 20. Cave & Inside Wizard's House

- PICK UP hairbrush (on the table in the right postcard in the bottom left corner) (`Brush`)

## 21. Mermaid & Balder

- USE `Medicine` on Balder
- PICK UP garbage (`Garbage`)
- USE `Brush` on Mermaid (`V.I.P. Pass`)

## 22. Underwater & Swamp

- USE `V.I.P. Pass` on crab (`Bracelet`)

## 23. Dragoncave & Beach

- USE `Bracelet` on Dragon (`Engraved Eye`)

## 24. Golden Door & Cave

- USE `Garbage` on Meep (`Elephant Egg`)
- USE `Engraved Eye` on golden door

## 25. Cauldron & Mountain Top

- USE `Fur` on Druid (`Gold Ring`)
- USE `Flint and Steel` on firewood
- USE `Clean Water` on cauldron (`Mug`)

## 26. Stump & Crystals

- USE `Gold Ring` on Angus (`Elephant Egg`)

## 27. Pink Elephant & Druids

- USE `Elephant Egg` on Elephant
- USE `Fur` on Druid (`Blue Crystal`)

## 28. Golden Door & Cave

- USE `Blue Crystal` on pedestal

## 29. Underwater & Swamp

- USE `Mug` on swamp (`Swamp Water`)

## 30. Stump & Crystals

- PICK UP sword (`Sword`)
- PICK UP elephant egg (`Elephant Egg`)
- PICK UP roasted pig (`Roasted Pig`)

## 31. Old Woman & Beach

- USE `Roasted Pig` on old woman (`Potion Recipe`)
- LOOK AT `Potion Recipe`

## 32. Pink Elephant & Druids

- USE `Elephant Egg` on elephant (`Tomato`)

## 33. Cave & Inside Wizard's House

- USE `Sword` on Walder (`Multicolored Quartz`)

## 34. Cauldron & Mountain Top

- USE `Salt` on cauldron
- USE `Fish Scales` on cauldron
- USE `Berries` on cauldron
- USE `Firebrand Leaves` on cauldron
- USE `Goblin's Hair` on cauldron
- USE `Tomato` on cauldron
- USE `Swamp Water` on cauldron (`Potion`)

## 35. Stump & Crystals

- USE `Multicolored Quartz` on pedestal
- USE book portal

## 36. Malodoro's Cave

- TALK TO Malodoro
- USE `Potion` on Malodoro
- PICK UP magic wand (`Magic Wand`)
- USE `Magic Wand` on spells book

## 37. Old Woman & Beach

- TALK TO old woman

## 38. Malodoro's Cave

- USE `Magic Wand` on spells book
