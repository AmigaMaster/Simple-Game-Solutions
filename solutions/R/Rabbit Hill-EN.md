## 1. Scene of Accident

- PICK UP large stone (on the lower street) (`Large Stone`)
- USE `Large Stone` on car door
- USE broken window
  - PICK UP wallet (`Wallet`)
  - PICK UP car keys (`Car Keys`)
  - EXIT
- USE `Wallet`
  - PICK UP coin (`Coin`)
  - PICK UP debit card (`Debit Card`)
  - EXIT
- EXIT east
