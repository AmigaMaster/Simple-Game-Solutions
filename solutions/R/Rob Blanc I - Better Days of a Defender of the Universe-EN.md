# Rob Blanc I - Better Days of a Defender of the Universe - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 06.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/swizzle_demu_Rb)

## 1. Top Corridor

- EXIT left

## 2. Top Left Corridor

- USE toolbox **2 times** (`Spanner`, `Screwdriver`)
- EXIT down

## 3. Left Corridor

- EXIT left

## 4. Bottom Left Corridor

- EXIT right

## 5. Bottom Corridor

- EXIT right

## 6. Bottom Right Corridor

- TALK TO droid
  - "I'm the Captain." (`Keycard`)
- USE door

### 6.1. Workshop

- PICK UP scalpel (`Laser Scalpel`)
- EXIT left

## 7. Bottom Right Corridor

- EXIT up

## 8. Right Corridor

- EXIT left

## 9. Top Right Corridor

- USE `Keycard` on door

### 9.1. Captain's Quarter

- PICK UP coil (`Coil`)
- USE `Laser Scalpel` on hand (`Hand`)
- EXIT left, down, right

## 10. Bottom Right Corridor

- USE `Coil` on droid
- USE `Screwdriver` on dead droid (`Vacuum Cleaner`, `Crystal`)
- USE droid
- EXIT left **2 times**

## 11. Bottom Left Corridor

- USE ladder

## 12. Engine Room

- USE `Spanner` on dilemma
- USE `Vacuum Cleaner` on slime
- USE `Crystal` on connector
- USE ladder

## 13. Bottom Left Corridor

- EXIT right

## 14. Bottom Corridor

- USE `Hand` on panel
- TALK TO hologram
