# Rob: The Massacre Of Chainsawness - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 05.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

## 1. Red Room

- EXIT north

## 2. Corridor

- EXIT east (5/5)
- EXIT south

## 3. White Room

- TALK TO man (`Fuel`) (5/10)
- PICK UP chainsaw (`Chainsaw`) (5/15)
- USE `Fuel` on `Chainsaw` (5/20)
- USE `Chainsaw` on man (5/25)
- EXIT south

## 4. Corridor

- USE `Chainsaw` on **all three** doors (5+5+5/40)
- EXIT west

## 5. Red Room

- USE `Chainsaw` on crack in the wall (5/45)

## 6. Pink Room

- USE `Chainsaw` on man with blue top (5/50)
- USE `Chainsaw` on man with yellow top (5/55)
- USE left wall (on the upper left part of the screen) (2/57)
  - **NOTE:** You can repeat this step and get more than 77 points in total
- EXIT north

## 7. Attic

- USE `Chainsaw` on all three persons (5+5+5/72)
- USE door opening in the back wall  (5/77)
