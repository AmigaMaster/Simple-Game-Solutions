# Rob Blanc II - Planet of the Pasteurised Pestilence - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 07.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/swizzle_demu_Rb2)

## 1. Elevator

- TALK TO Paul
- USE `Book` on Paul
- USE wall
- TALK TO Paul
  - "Bad news..."
  - "Help me..."

## 2. Castle

### 2.1. Throne Room

- PICK UP helmet (`Helmet`)
- EXIT south

### 2.2. Entrance

- EXIT east

## 3. Right Side of Castle

- PICK UP rope (`Rope`)
- Walk southwest

### 3.1. Entrance

- EXIT south

## 4. Western Street

- EXIT north

## 5. Alley

- TALK TO drainpipe
- PICK UP credit card (`Credit Card`)
- EXIT west

## 6. Western Street

- Use the doors

### 6.1. Karina Xaveloy

- PICK UP slumpfish oil (the grey bottle) (`Slumpfish Oil`)
- PICK UP oxygen tank (`Oxygen Tank`)
- EXIT south, west, north

## 7. Castle

### 7.1. Throne Room

- EXIT east

### 7.2. Nursery

- PICK UP milk (`Milk`)
- USE `Slumpfish Oil` on `Milk` (`Milk with Oil`)
- USE `Milk with Oil` on baby
- USE door

## 8. Western Street

- EXIT east

## 9. Eastern Street

- USE another door

### 9.1. Doctor

- USE `Milk with Oil` on microscope

## 10. Castle

### 10.1. Throne Room

- TALK TO king
  - "Good news, Kingy..."
- EXIT south, west

## 11. Farm

- TALK TO farmer
- USE shed (`Coveralls`)
- USE `Coveralls` on `Helmet` (`Radiation Suit`)
- USE `Oxygen Tank` on `Radiation Suit` (`Diving Suit`)
- EXIT east, west

## 12. Lake

- USE `Diving Suit` on Rob
- USE lake

### 12.1. Underwater

- USE `Rope` on canister

## 13. Lake

- USE rope

## 14. Eastern Street

- EXIT east

## 15. Desert

- EXIT south

## 16. Crash Site

- PICK UP sturdy rod (`Sturdy Rod`)
- TALK TO Paul

## 17. Space

- PICK UP chalk (`Chalk`)
- USE `Chalk` on constellation
- PICK UP crowbar (`Crowbar`)
- USE `Crowbar` on portal
- USE portal
- TALK TO Paul

## 18. White Room

- EXIT north

## 19. Garden

- PICK UP newspaper (`Newspaper`)
- LOOK AT `Newspaper` (`Key`)
- EXIT south

## 20. White Room

- USE `Key` on door no. 4

### 20.1. Computer Room

- USE drawer (`Skeleton Key`)
- EXIT west

## 21. White Room

- USE `Skeleton Key` on door no 2.

### 21.1. Training Room

- PICK UP energy drink (`Stoca Zola Sport`)
- USE `Stoca Zola Sport` on Paul
- PICK UP weights (It does  not work)
- EXIT north
- USE `Skeleton Key` on door no. 1

## 22. Bedroom

- EXIT west

### 22.1. Bathroom

- USE cistern lid
- PICK UP toothbrush
