## 1. Outside Castle Entrance

- >**EASTER EGG:**
  >- TALK TO Graham until he gives a reference to King's Quest 5 - Chance is 1 in 15
- EXIT west
- >**DEATH:**
  >- Fall into the moat

## 2. Outside Castle, West Path

- EXIT west

## 3. Shady Forest Clearing

- WALK above the stone
- PUSH stone (2/2)
- TAKE hole (`Silver dagger`) (5/7)
- EXIT north

## 4. Oak Tree

- USE big tree (2/9)

### 4.1. Inside Oak Tree

- Carefully walk over the branch
- TAKE egg (`Golden egg`) (6/15)
- Jump down

## 7. Well

- USE `Silver dagger` with rope (`Wooden bucket`) (2/17)
- USE crank
- USE rope (2/19)

### 7.1. Inside Well

- USE water **2 times** (4/23)

### 7.2. Underwater

- >**DEATH:**
  >- Wait and you'll drown
- >**EASTER EGG:**
  >- Try to quit the game while you are underwater
- SWIM to the northwest hole
- USE path next to the hole to exit the water
- USE `Wooden bucket` with hole (`Wooden bucket with water`) (2/25)
- EXIT west (1/26)

### 7.3. Dragon Cave

- >**DEATH:**
  >- Move to the dragon **2 times**
- USE `Wooden bucket with water` with dragon (5/31)
- TAKE mirror (`Magic mirror`) (8/39)
- EXIT west

### 7.4. Vacant Cave

- EXIT northwest (2/41)

## 29. Gingerbread House

- USE house (2/73)
- USE door **2 times**

### 29.1. Inside Gingerbread House

- >**EASTER EGG:**
  >- LOOK AT smoke from the cauldron
- WALK to the bedroom
- WAIT
- Carefully walk beside the witch
- USE witch (7/80)
- USE cabinet (2/82)
- TAKE cheese (`Swiss cheese`) (2/84)
- TAKE note (from the table beside the bed) (`Note`) (1/85)
- LOOK AT `Note` (2/87)
- >**EASTER EGG:**
  >- LOOK AT `Note` - Chance of 1 in 15 to get a different message
- EXIT house
