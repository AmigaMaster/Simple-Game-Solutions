## 1. Park

### 1.1. Cliff

- PICK UP rope (`Rope`)
- TALK TO man
- PICK UP Stones (`Stone`)
- LOOK AT large rock (`Stick`)
- USE `Rope` on man **3 times**
- PICK UP bottle (`Bottle`)
- USE `Stick` on cliff
- USE `Bottle` on hole
- USE bottle

### 1.2. Top of Cliff

- TALK TO drunk man
- LOOK AT sign (on the noticeboard) (New Location: Bar)
- LOOK AT job offers **until you get the number of Matt, the recycler** (New Phone Number)
- EXIT east

## 2. Jeff's Bar

- TALK TO barman (`Red Glass`, `Yellow Glas`, `Vodka Bottle`)
  - **NOTE:** The red glass is 12ml, the yellow one 5ml. Now you must fill the red glass with 11ml:
    1. `Vodka Bottle` on `Red Glass` (12ml)
    2. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (-5 = 7ml)
    3. `Yellow Glass` on `Vodka Bottle`
    4. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (-5 = 2ml)
    5. `Yellow Glass` on `Vodka Bottle`
    6. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (Red  0ml / Yellow 2ml)
    7. `Vodka Bottle` on `Red Glass` (Red 12ml / Yellow 2ml)
    8. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (Red  9ml / Yellow 5ml)
    9. `Yellow Glass` on `Vodka Bottle` (Red 9ml / Yellow 0ml)
    10. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (Red 4ml / Yellow 5ml)
    11. `Yellow Glass` on `Vodka Bottle` (Red 4ml / Yellow 0ml)
    12. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (Red 0ml / Yellow 4ml)
    13. `Vodka Bottle` on `Red Glass` (Red 12ml / Yellow 4ml)
    14. `Red Glass` on `Yellow Glass` (Red 11ml / Yellow 5ml)
- USE `Red Glass` on barman (`Coupon`)
- PICK UP matchbox (on the left corner of the bar) (`Matchbox`)
- USE door

## 17. Korsow

### 17.1. Harbour

### 17.2. The Old Well

- **ATTENTION:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
- LOOK AT songo
  - LOOK AT right eye
  - USE `Binoculars` on right eye
- **QUICK:**
  - WALK behind (right) pillar
- WAIT until Jazz arrives and WAIT again until he looks to the left side, then:
  - USE `Stone` on Jazz (`Radio`)
- PICK UP gun (behind the bush) (`Gun`)
- USE `Rope` on Jazz
- USE `Gun` on Jazz (New Location: ALBA Chemical)
- USE `Radio` on Jazz
- EXIT east
