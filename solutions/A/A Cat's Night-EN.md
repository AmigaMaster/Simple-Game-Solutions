# A Cat's Night - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 03.08.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de), heltenjon (from the AGS forum)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/a-cats-night)

## 1. Office Area

- PICK UP handle (under the sink) (`Ladle`)
- TALK TO Morgan (on the roof)
  - "You look a bit agitated."
  - "That's not the way to behave."
  - "Bullies are just insecure."
  - "By treating him kindly and sincerely."

## 2. Internal Area

### 2.1. House

- PICK UP keyring (from the door) (`Some Key`)
- PICK UP fan (`Fan`)
- USE cat flap

## 3. Internal Area

- USE pipes (right of Elwood) (`Adjustable Pipe`)
- TALK TO Jack
- EXIT northwest

## 4. Wooded Area

- TALK TO Orienter (`Stone`)
- USE `Adjustable Pipe` on beehive (`Honey`)
- USE `Honey` on `Stone` (`Nugget`)
- EXIT northwest

## 5. Boundary

- PICK UP shears (in the bottom left corner) (`Shears`)
- TALK TO thieving magpie
- USE `Nugget` on thieving magpie
- USE hole in the tree
  - TALK TO squirrelkins
  - EXIT
- EXIT east **2 times**, southeast

## 6. Office Area

- ENTER right house

### 6.1. Kittens House

- USE bucket (`Sand`)
- TALK TO Camilla (`Cotton Bud`)
- USE `Fan` on hanging toy
- PICK UP strange toy (`Strange Toy`)
- EXIT

## 7. Office Area

- ENTER large cage

### 7.1. Large Cage

- TALK TO Mamba
- TALK TO Giovannone
- USE `Sand` on puddle (under the fountain on the right) (`Clay`)

### 7.2. Kittens House

- USE `Clay` on lock (of the left door) (`Model of Key`)

## 8. Office Area

- USE `Shears` on counterweight (above the yellow bags)
- USE `Strange Toy` on swing door
- ENTER go inside

## 9. Office Backside

- PICK UP hairspray (`Hairspray`)
- EXIT north

## 10. Office Area

- EXIT south (on the west side)

## 11. Storeroom

- PICK UP straw (`Straw`)
- TALK TO Bettina
- TALK TO Anita

## 12. Internal Area

- USE `Straw` on Elwood
- PICK UP hair ball (`Hair Ball`)
- USE `Hairspray` on `Hair Ball` (`Sticky Hair Ball`)

## 13. Office Area

### 13.1. Large Cage

- TALK TO Giovannone (`Spoon`)
- USE `Some Key` on Mamba (`Modified Key`)

### 13.2. Kittens House

- USE `Modified Key` on lock

#### 13.2.1. Office

- USE `Ladle` on cupboard (in the lower right corner)
- USE light switch (`Sweet`)
- USE central compartment (the middle part of the cupboard on the right side) (`Sticky Tape`)

## 14. Internal Area

### 14.1. House

- USE `Sticky Tape` on damaged wire
- USE hole (`Empty Can`)
- USE `Empty Can` on `Cotton Bud` (`Lubricating Kit`)

## 15. Storeroom

- USE `Lubricating Kit` on door
- ENTER Storeroom

### 15.1. Storage Room

- USE `Spoon` on tin of paint
- USE `Sweet` on tin of paint (`Disguised Sweet`)
- PICK UP lawnmower (in the lower right corner) (`Lawnmower`)

## 16. Wooded Area

- USE `Lawnmower` on clump of grass

## 17. Boundary

- USE hole in the tree
  - USE `Disguised Sweet` on hole
- PICK UP remote control (`Remote Control`)

## 18. Wooded Area

- USE `Remote Control` on toy lawnmower

## 19. Boundary

- USE `Nugget` on thieving magpie

## 20. Wooded Area

- USE `Sticky Hair Ball` on crane bucket
