# ~airwave~ : I Fought the Law, and the Law One - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 30.01.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/i-fought-the-law-and-the-law-one)

## 1. Radio Station

### 1.1. Outside

- EXIT west

## 2. Ometa Bar

### 2.1. Outside Bar

- PICK UP nails (`Nails`)
- TALK TO Audrey
- ENTER bar

### 2.2. Bar

- TALK TO Kim
- PICK UP bottle cap (`Bottle Cap`)
- TALK TO Jared (`Document`)
- EXIT west **2 times**

## 3. Grill

- TALK TO Lady Ophelia
- USE bell (on top of the food display)
- TALK TO Laurence
- PICK UP basket (on the ground left of Lady Ophelia) (`Basket`)
- EXIT west

## 4. Ometa Bar

### 4.1. Bar

- USE cocktail menu
- TALK TO Kim
  - "Can I get a drink?"
  - "Can you tell me about these cocktails?"
  - We need a cocktail which contains mango
  - "Thanks for the help."
  - "Can I have a Tangy Time-warp?"
  - "Could you add some ouzo?" (`Cocktail`)

## 5. Grill

- USE `Cocktail` on Lady Ophelia
- PICK UP samples (from the table on the right) (`Fat on a Stick`)
- USE `Fat on a Stick` on Lady Ophelia

## 6. Caravan

- TALK TO Damon
- USE `Fat on a Stick` on Damon
  - As soon as the screen is shaking, CLICK
- USE bag of instruments (`Horn`)
- PICK UP pliers (behind Damon) (`Pliers`)

## 7. Grill

- USE `Horn` on Lady Ophelia
- TALK TO Lady Ophelia
- USE `Document` on Lady Ophelia

## 8. Ometa Bar

### 8.1. Bar

- TALK TO Jared

### 8.2. Outside Bar

- TALK TO Audrey

## 9. Radio Station

### 9.1. Outside

- USE bottles (`Bottle Caps`)
- USE `Pliers` on cable (right next to the wheel) (`Cable`)
- ENTER station

### 9.2. Station

- USE trash can (`Bottle Caps`)
- USE `Bottle Caps` on `Basket` (`Unfinished Tambourine`)
- USE `Nails` on `Unfinished Tambourine` (`Tambourine`)

## 10. Ometa Bar

### 10.1. Outside Bar

- USE `Cable` on Audrey

## 11. Grill

- USE `Cable` on Laurence (`Boiled Cable`)

## 12. Ometa Bar

### 12.1. Outside

- USE `Boiled Cable` on Audrey
- USE `Tambourine` on Rasputin

### 12.2. Bar

- TALK TO Kim
