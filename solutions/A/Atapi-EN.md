# Atapi - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 30.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [Adventure Game Studio](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/play/game/1171-atapi)

## 1. Hut

- USE `Pendant` on Boma
  - **NOTE:** She needs smartweed, cobweb, cattail and kindling
- EXIT northeast

## 2. River

- TALK TO Kiri
  - "Kiri, come with me."
- EXIT west

## 3. Hut

- EXIT south

## 4. White Stone

- EXIT south

## 5. Southern Crossway

- PICK UP stick (in the southern path) (`Stick`)
- EXIT north

## 6. White Stone

- EXIT east

## 7. Corner

- EXIT south **2 times**

## 8. Path with Bushes

- PICK UP stone (`Stone`)
- EXIT south

## 9. Path with Cobwebs

- TALK TO Kiri
  - "Kiri, can you get rid of this web?"
- USE `Stick` on Kiri
- TALK TO Kiri
  - "Kiri, please give me back that stick." (`Stick with Cobweb`)
- USE `Stick with Cobweb` on yourself (`Stick`, `Cobweb`)
- EXIT southeast

## 10. Forest Road

- EXIT south

## 11. Darker Forest

- PICK UP bowl (`Metal Bowl`)
- EXIT east

## 12. Bonfire Place

- USE bonfire (in the middle of the screen) (`Dry sticks`)
- EXIT south, west, north

## 13. Swamp

- PICK UP cattail (the brown plant in the upper right part of the screen) (`Cattail`)
- EXIT north

## 14. Northern Swamp

- EXIT southeast

## 15. Eastern Swamp

- PICK UP cattail (in the lower left corner of the screen) (`Cattail`)
- EXIT west

## 16. Northern Swamp

- EXIT north

## 17. Northwestern Swamp

- PICK UP scouring rush (in the bottom left corner) (`Scouring Rush`)
- EXIT west

## 18. River

- USE `Stone` on snake
- EXIT north

## 19. Dead Tree

- PICK UP smartweed (in the bottom right corner) (`Smartweed`)
- EXIT south, west

## 20. Hut

- USE `Smartweed` on Boma
- USE `Cobweb` on Boma
- USE `Dry Sticks` on Boma
- USE `Cattail` on Boma
- EXIT south, east, south **3 times**, southeast, south, east

## 21. Bonfire Place

- USE `Cloth` on Kiri (`Cloth with Blood`, `Atapi Stone`)

## 22. Southern Crossway

- EXIT north

## 23. White Stone

- USE `Atapi Stone` on white stone
  - USE the right arrow icon to change to the second symbol bar
  - "help" (2nd symbol)
  - OK
    - **NOTE:** You need magic earth, metal, water, air, wood and fire
- EXIT north

## 24. Hut

- PICK UP dirt (from the ground) (`Dirt`)
- EXIT south

## 25. White Stone

- USE `Dirt` on white stone (`Magic Earth`)
- EXIT north, east

## 26. River

- USE `Atapi Stone` on river
  - "yes"
- USE `Metal Bowl` on river (`Metal Bowl with magic Water`)
- EXIT north

## 27. Dead Tree

- USE `Atapi Stone` on dead tree
  - "yes"
- USE `Atapi Stone` on dead tree
  - "yes" (again)
- USE `Metal Bowl with magic Water` on dead tree (`Metal Bowl`)
  - "help"
- USE `Stick` on dead tree (`Magic Wood`)
- EXIT south, west

## 28. Hut

- TALK TO Kiri
  - "Did Boma tell you what is happening?"
- EXIT south, east, south **2 times**

## 29. Path with Bushes

- **QUICK:**
  - USE `Atapi Stone` on wind (the moving bushes on the left)
    - "Little"
- USE `Atapi Stone` on wind
  - "male"
- USE `Atapi Stone` on wind
  - "north"
- USE `Atapi Stone` on wind
  - "take"
- USE `Atapi Stone` on wind
  - "bad" (`Magic Air`) (there is no inventory item for this)
- EXIT south, east

## 30. Forest Road

- WALk into the sunshine
- USE `Atapi Stone` on sunshine
  - "help"
- EXIT south, east

## 31. Bonfire Place

- TALK TO man **2 times**
- EXIT south, west **2 times**, north

## 32. Path with Bushes

- USE `Atapi Stone` on wind
  - **NOTE:** If the wind isn't blowing, keep walking around an return here once for a while until it blows
  - "plural water mortal"
- EXIT south, east, south, east

## 33. Bonfire Place

- TALK TO man
  - "I know where your caravan is."
- USE `Atapi Stone` on man (`Axe`)
- USE `Metal Bowl` on `Axe` (`Magic Metal Bowl`)
- EXIT south, west

## 34. Forest Road

- USE `Cattail` on sunshine
- USE `Scouring Rush` on `Magic Metal Bowl` (`Gleaming Magic Metal Bowl`)
- USE `Gleaming Magic Metal Bowl` on sunshine (`Magic Fire`)
- EXIT west, north **3 times**, west, north, east

## 35. River

- USE `Gleaming Magic Metal Bowl` on river (`Magic Water in Magic Bowl`)
- USE `Atapi Stone` on river
  - "big water atapi"
- EXIT east **three times**

## 36. Northeastern Swamp

- USE water

## 37. Pond

- EXIT south

## 38. Grass

- USE `Magic Water in Magic Bowl` on small tree
- USE `Magic Fire` on small tree
- USE `Magic Wood` on small tree
- USE `Magic Earth` on small tree
- TALK TO small tree

## 39. Water Atapi

- USE `Cloth with Blood` on water Atapi (`Vial`)

## 40. Northeastern Swamp

- EXIT south **three times**

## 41. Forest Road

- USE `Axe` on wood goddess
- EXIT west, north

## 42. Path with Bushes

- USE `Vial` on wind
