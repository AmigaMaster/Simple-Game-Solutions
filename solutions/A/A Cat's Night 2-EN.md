# A Cat's Night 2 - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 15.08.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/a-cats-night-2-orazio-goes-to-town)

## 1. Orazio's House

### 1.1. Balcony

- TALK TO Pamperina
- PICK UP pot of glue (on the small table on the left) (`Glue`)
- PICK UP piece of string (on the scratching post) (`Piece of String`)
- PICK UP broom handle (the red one) (`Broom Handle`)
- PICK UP peg basket (on the right) (`Pegs`)
- USE `Broom Handle` on `Pegs` (`Gripping Handle`)
- USE `Gripping Handle` on thingummy (in the top left flower box)
- PICK UP fishing reel (`Fishing Reel`)
- USE `Fishing Reel` on `Piece of String` (`Reel loaded with String`)
- USE `Reel loaded with String` on `Glue` (`Reel with Glue on it`)
- USE `Reel with Glue on it` on `Gripping Handle` (`Clothes-Grabber`)
- USE `Clothes-Grabber` on washing
- USE `Pegs` on heap of clothes

## 2. Street

- USE cement-mixer (`Fresh Cement`)
- USE `Fresh Cement` on exhaust pipe (`Hardened Cement`)
- USE `Hardened Cement` on pulley (at t he top of the screen)
- USE `Clothes-Grabber` on control box (`Map` in the menu screen)

## 12. Orazio's House

### 12.1. Street

- The knotted clothes (rope) hang from the balcony.
- USE `Boxing Glove` on boot (of the blue car)
- USE `Pipe` on gas cylinders
- PICK UP fishing net (`Fishing Net`)
- USE `TV Aerial` on manhole cover
- EXIT down to the sewer

### 12.2. Sewer

- TALK TO Agamennone
  - "Is there anything I can do for you?"
  - "Do you want to hear the story of my life?"
  - Select any answers until Agamennone fells asleep
- USE `Fishing Net` on seafood? (in the water on the left side of the flow of water) (`Seafood`)
- EXIT up the ladder

## 13. Rooftops

### 13.1. Lower Rooftops

## 14. San Domenico Museums

## 22. Photo Booth

- TALK TO Napoleone (behind the booth) (`Heap of Feathers`, `Water Bottle`)
- PICK UP bum bag (in the booth) (`Bum Bag`)
- LOOK AT `Bum Bag` (`Folding Nail File`, `Pack of Cards`)
- TALK TO Napoleone
- USE `Water Bottle` on pot of glue (in the lower left)
- USE `Heap of Feather` on diluted glue (`Sticky Feathers`)
- USE `Folding Nail File` on `Fishing Net` (`Stick`, `Net`)
- USE `Folding Nail File` on drainpipe (a small hotspot above the bicycle on the building in the background, it's quite hard to spot because of the big icon) (`Pipe`)
- USE `Pipe` bicycle wheel
- USE pipe stuck in the wheel (`Squashed Tube`)
- USE `Pack of Cards` on Napoleone

## 23. Pigeon Base

- USE `Plank of Wood` on fire extinguisher
- USE `Net` on `Metal Weights` (`Escape-Proof Net`)
- USE `Escape-Proof Net` on catapult
- USE `Stick` on brick (above the catapult on the metal cage) (`Official Document`, New Location: `Theatre`)

## 38. McTruff Palace

### 38.1. Outside

- USE `Iron Bar` on grating

### 38.2. Secretary's Office

- PICK UP nameplate (`Nameplate`)
- PICK UP mop (`Mop`)
- USE `Mop` on mobile phone (on the floor) (`Mobile Phone`)
- USE `Nameplate` on `Mobile Phone`

### 38.3. Office

- PICK UP cover (`Metal Cover`)
- PICK UP bottle of wine (`Bottle of Wine`)
- USE `Metal Cover` on laser beams
- USE `Bottle of Wine` wine on water (of the fountain)
- PICK UP shiny object (at the bottom of the fountain) (`Diamond Ring`)
- USE `Diamond Ring` on glass case (of the bottom wall cabinet)
- PICK UP tribal sculpture (`Tribal Sculpture`)
- USE `Tribal Sculpture` on laser beams
- USE newspaper (on the desk)
  - **NOTE:** The date `7 May 2012`
- USE keypad (in the desk)
  - ENTER `705`
