- TRADE pornography at Tarabis 6 with baconburgers at Cary 7 until you have at least 250 credits

## 5. Optional Mission: Galactic Database

The target is to scan 42 objects. To start the mission:

### 5.1. Ship

- TRAVEL TO Protected Zone (you already should be here)
- ASK Hole about bulletin board
- WARP to Remus 3
- Open communications to the Grimbace University
  - "We're interested in the researcher job."
- WARP to Frumious Prime
- BUY the ship scanner from Dirty Laura's Dealership

- You can now:
  - on your ship, tell Hole to scan each planet
  - scan ships during a fight in each of the three systems (Go hunting for a fight)
- See the following walkthrough for most of the 42 scan locations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- WARP to Frumious Prime
- Tell Hole to scan the location (1)
- Tell Eric to BUY at least 15 redshirts from Redshirt Security (BUY 3x5 for 100 Credits each)
  - **NOTE:** If you want to fulfil all optional missions, you need about 30 redshirts, so you need 300 more credits
- BUY Chocolate
- BUY tractor BEAM, laser upgrade from Dirty Laura's Dealership, keep at least 1100 credits
- WARP to wormhole
- Tell Hole to scan the location (2)
- Open communications with wormhole authority
- PAY fee
- WARP to Free Zone wormhole and and sell chocolate
- Tell Hole to scan the location (3)
- Open communications with wormhole authority
- PAY fee
- WARP to Bloody Towel
- Tell Hole to scan the location (Bloody Towel) (4)
- **NOTE:** You need about 1000 credits before you can continue
- BEAM down a redshirt
