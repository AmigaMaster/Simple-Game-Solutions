## 2. Day Two

### 2.1. First Floor

#### 2.1.1. Dining Room

- TALK TO Philip

#### 2.3.1. Hallway

#### 2.5.1. Backyard

## 3. Day Three

### 3.1. Backyard

- USE car (`Lockpicks`)
- USE `Lockpicks` on (north) door

#### 3.1.1. Workshop

- PICK UP pickaxe (`Pickaxe`)
- PICK UP saw (`Saw`)
- USE door

### 3.2. Second Floor

#### 3.2.1. Hallway

- USE `Lockpicks` on (fourth) door
- USE (fourth) door

#### 3.2.2. Bedroom

- USE wardrobe
- PICK UP book (`(green) Diary`)
- USE window
- USE open window
  - LOOK AT tree
  - USE (left) window
- USE door

#### 3.2.3. Library

- TALK TO Jim

### 3.3. Backyard

- Wait until Jim arrives
- USE `Saw` on tree

### 3.4. Second Floor

#### 3.4.1. Bedroom

- USE window
  - USE `Grolly` on loose tile
  - USE east window

#### 3.4.2. Nursery

- PICK UP teddy (`Teddy`)
- USE nightstand (`(red) Diary`)
- LOOK AT `(green) Diary`
- LOOK AT `(red) Diary`
- LOOK AT `Article`

#### 3.4.3. Library

- PICK UP (black) books (at the right side of the second shelf) (`Book`)
- LOOK AT `Book`
- USE `Cord` on `Stick` (`Cord + Stick`)
- USE `Cord + Stick` on `Teddy` (`Bear + Stick`)

### 3.5. First Floor

#### 3.5.1. Kitchen

- USE (second) cabinet
- USE `Bear + Stick` on salt (`Salty Bear + Stick`)

### 3.6. Second Floor

#### 3.6.1. Nursery

- USE `Salty Bear + Stick` on bed
- LOOK AT `Salty Bear + Stick`

### 3.7. First Floor

#### 3.7.1. Dining Room

- LOOK AT `Salty Bear + Stick`

#### 3.7.2. Kitchen

- LOOK AT `Salty Bear + Stick`
- USE `Pickaxe` on wall
- USE hole

### 3.8. Basement

- LOOK AT `Salty Bear + Stick` and follow the directions until you reached the spot (it's in the southeast corner) (`Teddy`)
- USE area
- EXIT upstairs

### 3.9. First Floor

#### 3.9.1. Trophy Room

- PICK UP idol

## 4. Day Four

### 4.1. Backyard

#### 4.1.1. Workshop

- USE door
- TALK TO window **2 times**
  - "What makes you think..."
  - "I was possessed..."
  - "It possessed me just like..."
  - "I'm delirious..."
- PICK UP tie (`Lockpick`)
- USE `Lockpick` on door
- USE door

### 4.2. Second Floor

#### 4.2.1. Hallway

- **ATTENTION:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
- USE (east) door

#### 4.2.2. Bathroom

- When the killer steps on the rug:
  - USE rug (`Welding Mask`, `Apron`, `Machete`)

## 5. Day Five

### 5.1. Second Floor

#### 5.1.1. Bedroom

- TALK TO Jim

#### 5.1.2. Library

- PICK UP (black) books (at the right side of the second shelf) (`Black Magic Book`)
- LOOK AT `Black Magic Book`

### 5.2. First Floor

#### 5.2.1. Living Room

- PICK UP scissors (`Scissors`)
- USE `Scissors` on `Apron` (`Strip`)
- USE `Strip` on `Machete` (`Machete + Strip`)
- USE `Machete + Strip` on `Welding Mask` (`Mask + Machete`)

#### 5.2.2. Kitchen

- USE `Mask + Machete` on salt (`Salty Mask + Machete`)
- USE hole

### 5.3. Basement

- USE `Salty Mask + Machete` on chains

### 5.4. First Floor

#### 5.4.1. Kitchen

- LOOK AT `Salty Mask + Machete`

### 5.5. Second Floor

#### 5.5.1. Hallway

- LOOK AT `Salty Mask + Machete`
- USE (east) door

#### 5.5.2. Bathroom

- LOOK AT `Salty Mask + Machete` and follow the directions of the salty mask (it's right next to the rug)
- USE `Scissors` on `Salty Mask + Machete` (`Welding Mask`, `Machete`)
- USE `Machete` on tiles
- PICK UP body (`Remains`)

### 5.6. First Floor

#### 5.6.1. Trophy Room

- USE `Remains` on floor
- USE `Welding Mask` on remains

#### 5.6.2. Living Room

- TALK TO Simone
  - Send her to the trophy room

### 5.7. Second Floor

#### 5.7.1. Bedroom

- TALK TO Jim
  - Send him to the trophy room

### 5.8. First Floor

#### 5.8.1. Trophy Room

- PICK UP rifle
  - USE Rifle on Simone
- USE `Teddy` on Jim
- USE `Black Magic Book` on Trilby
