# Donnie Darko - The Adventure Game - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 14.09.2022 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/donnie-darko-the-adventure-game)

## 1. Donnie's House

### 1.1. Bed Room

- PICK UP Letter (`Letter`)
- USE Drawer (`Paperclip`)
- USE Door

### 1.2. Entrance to your House

- EXIT west

## 2. Sparrow Residence

- USE `Letter` with Mailbox
- EXIT east

## 3. Aero Theatre

- LOOK AT Marquee
- TALK TO Theatre Guy about everything
- EXIT west

## 4. Middlesex Church

### 4.1. Entrance to the Church

- USE Church Entrance

### 4.2. Main Hall

- TALK TO Kitty
  - "These films shouldn't be banned!"
  - "There's nothing offensive about them!"
  - "Yeah... have you?"
- USE Door

### 4.3. Back Room

- PICK UP Film Reels (`Film Reels`)
- EXIT the Church

## 5. Aero Theatre

- USE `Film Reels` with Theatre Guy

## 6. Middlesex Mall

### 6.1. Entrance to the Mall

- USE Mall Entrance

### 6.2. Entrance Hall

- TALK TO Shopkeeper (`Pair of Pliers`)
- USE Plastic Plant (`Plastic Twig`)
- EXIT west

### 6.3. Shops

- LOOK AT Barricade
- USE `Paperclip` with `Plastic Twig` (`Makeshift Hook`)
- USE `Makeshift Hook` with `Pair of Pliers` (`Extended Hook`)
- USE `Extended Hook` with Barricade
