### 11.2. Time Machine

- LOOK AT control panel
  - LOOK AT `Rhyme`
    - **NOTE:** The colors and events. This is a quite hard puzzle to solve.
  1. "On ferry's deck no need for four winds" is the invention of hot air balloon by the Montgolfier brothers in 1783
     - SELECT `A.D.`
     - USE 2 orange (orange) buttons
     - USE 4 red (strawberry) buttons (the bottom green button on the left lights up)
  2. Is Brutus the Demon
     - SELECT `B.C.`
     - USE first purple (plum) switch
     - USE yellow (lemon) switch **3 times**
     - USE first purple (plum) switch **again** (to close it)
     - USE second purple switch
     - USE yellow switch **3 times** (each purple plum now have three yellow lemons)
  3. Beowulf kills Grendel
     - SELECT `A.D.`
     - USE 4 green (mint) buttons and levers
     - USE orange on the wheel button
  4. The Globe (Shakespeare's Globe Theatre, built in London in 1599 A.D.)
     - SELECT `A.D.`
     - USE yellow on the wheel button
     - USE right red on the wheel button
     - USE bottom red on the wheel button
     - USE left red on the wheel button
- TALK TO Mac Geyser
  - "Where is the well located, exactly?" (`Note Pad`)
  - "Where did you say you lost your hammer?"
- LOOK AT `Note Pad`

### 30.3. Cave

- LOOK AT balance
  - Under the scale, there are different colored weights
  - They have Romanian letters (numbers):
    1. White  = VII   = 7
    2. Yellow = XI    = 11
    3. Green  = XIII  = 13
    4. Purple = XXIII = 23
    5. Red    = XXXI  = 31
    6. Orange = LV    = 55
    7. Blue   = LXX   = 70
    8. Gray   = LXXX  = 80
  - You have to place 4 weights on each side and they must be in balance
  - All weights are 290 / 2 = 145 on each side
  - Place `yellow, purple, red, gray` and `white, green, orange, blue`
- EXIT east
