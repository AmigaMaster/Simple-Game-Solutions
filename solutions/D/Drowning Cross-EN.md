# Drowning Cross - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 19.01.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [itch.io](https://origamihero.itch.io/drowning-cross)

## 1. Container Area

- PICK UP big wrench
  - "I'll just put it back in the toolbox" (`Big Wrench`)
- EXIT east

## 2. Construction Site

- PICK UP rope (`Rope`)
- EXIT southeast

## 3. Hill Path

- USE Leo's car
  - "(Check Back)"
  - "(Take Speakers)" (`Bluetooth Speaker`)
- EXIT southwest

## 4. City

- WALK TO home

### 4.1. Home

#### 4.1.1. Outside

- ENTER apartment

#### 4.1.2. Apartment

- USE wet trousers (`Leo's Locker Key`)
- PICK UP Jeremy's Phone (`Phone`)
- PICK UP white socks (`White Socks`)
- USE `Phone` on picture (`Photo of Leo & Jeremy`)
- USE Leo's Wallet (New Location: Bar)
- EXIT east **2 times**

### 4.2. Bar

#### 4.2.1. Street

- TALK TO bouncer
- USE `Photo of Leo & Jeremy` on bouncer
- ENTER bar

#### 4.2.2. Inside Bar

- TALK TO woman (New Location: Fast Food Place)
- PICK UP free lighters (`Big Box Lighter`)
- PICK UP piece of piping (from the floor) (`Piece of Piping`)
- USE drinks overview (`Thinkin' 'bout Cocktails`)
- USE `Thinkin' 'bout Cocktails` on woman
- USE `Leo's locker key` on lockers
- USE `Phone` on lockers (`Leo's Mom & Cross`)
- EXIT bar

### 4.3. Home

#### 4.3.1. Outside

- TALK TO old woman
- USE `Leo's Mom & Cross` on old woman (New Location: Old House)

### 4.4. Old House

#### 4.4.1. Outside

- USE `Big Wrench` on loose plank (`Plank`)
- USE `Piece of piping` on door
- USE `Big Wrench` on door
- ENTER door

#### 4.4.2. Inside House

- PICK UP paper stack **3 times** (`Old Newspapers`, `Obituary`)
- USE scribbles
- EXIT house

### 4.5. Fast Food Place

- PICK UP paper cup (`Paper Cup`)
- USE `Photo of Leo & Jeremy` on woman (`Jefferson Sinclaire`)

### 4.6. Home

#### 4.6.1. Apartment

- USE `Jefferson Sinclaire` on computer (New Location: Church)

### 4.7. Church Park

#### 4.7.1. Park

- ENTER church

#### 4.7.2. Church

- USE `Obituary` on Sinclaire
- EXIT church

#### 4.7.3. Park

- TALK TO teen
- USE `Bluetooth Speaker` on teen

#### 4.7.4. Church

- USE `Big Wrench` on door
- ENTER door

##### 4.7.4.1. Storage Room

- USE cupboard (`Angel Statue`)

### 4.8. Old House

#### 4.8.1. Outside

- EXIT east

#### 4.8.2. Forest Entrance

- USE `White Sockets` on broken branch
- EXIT north

#### 4.8.3. Forest Crossing

- USE `White Sockets` on (top) pole
- EXIT north

#### 4.8.4. Cliff Crossing

- USE `White Sockets` on pole (bottom right)
- EXIT southeast

#### 4.8.5. River

- USE `Paper Cup` on water (`Cup with Water`)
- USE `Angel Statue` on cross

## 5. Portal Room

- EXIT southeast

## 6. Bone Plateau

- USE `Plank` on well
- USE `Rope` on well
- USE well

### 6.1. Sea of Tears

- USE `Cup with Water` on lamp
- While the ghost turns away from you:
  - WALK into the next shadow area
- Again, wait until the ghost is on the left side and walk into the next shadow area
- USE `Paper Cup` on sea of tears (`Cup with Tears`)
- Make your way back
- USE well to EXIT up

## 7. Bone Plateau

- EXIT east

## 8. Bridge

- USE `Old Newspapers` on (brown) bowl
- USE `Big Box Lighter` on (brown) bowl
- USE `Cup with Tears` on (blue) bowl
- ENTER tower

## 9. Tower

### 9.1. Entrance Hall

- EXIT north (`What Happened?`)
- EXIT southeast, west, north

## 10. Portal Room

- USE portal and make your way back

## 11. City

- EXIT south, north, west

## 12. Container Area

- USE `What Happened?` on Jack
- Make your way back via the old house, through the forest and through the cross

## 13. Tower

### 13.1. Entrance Hall

- EXIT north
  - "I guess these folks..."
  - "Yo, come on, have a beer..."

### 13.2. Bedroom

- TALK TO Leo (he gets less translucent)
- EXIT south, southeast

## 14. Bridge

- USE `Leo` on statue (he turns to normal appearance)
- EXIT north **2 times**

## 15. Tower

### 15.1. Bedroom

- **NOTE:** SAVE YOUR GAME HERE
- EXIT north
