# Dr. Lutz and the Time Travel Machine - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 21.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/dr-lutz-and-the-time-travel-machine)

## 1. Basement

- USE rope (next to the window)
- USE fridge
- TAKE toast (`Toast with Jam`)
- TAKE bottle of water (`Bottle of Water`)
- LOOK AT `Toast with Jam` (`Wrapping Paper`)
- LOOK AT `Wrapping Paper`
  - **NOTE:** `First row to the ninth, back to the seventh then to the fourth`
- USE `Wrapping Paper` with books
  - **NOTE:** `Pour the source of life over the lifeless ground`
- USE `Bottle of Water` with flowerpot (`Code`)
- USE `Code` with safe (`Diamond`)
- USE `Diamond` with machine
- USE rope
- TAKE basket (`Sandwich`, `Apple`)
- LOOK AT basket (`Wrench`)
- USE `Wrench` with engine
- USE machine

## 2. Basement

- USE door
- EXIT upstairs

## 3. Entrance Hallway

- TALK TO kid
- EXIT upstairs

## 4. Second Floor

### 4.1. Hallway

- USE (right) door
- ENTER (right) door

#### 4.1.1. Nursery

- LOOK AT behind the screen (`Yo-Yo`)
- TAKE playing cards (`Playing Card`)
- USE loose board (on the floor)
- LOOK AT gap in the floor
  - USE cheese
  - CLOSE
- EXIT room

### 4.2. Hallway

- USE (left) door
- USE `Playing Card` with (left) door
- ENTER (left) door

#### 4.2.1. Bedroom

- LOOK AT pictures
  - **NOTE:** The top three pictures: `2` dogs, `1` horse, `3` cats
  - CLOSE
- EXIT room

## 5. Entrance Hallway

- USE `Yo-Yo` with kid (`Short Stick`)

## 6. Second Floor

### 6.1. Nursery

- LOOK AT gap in the floor
  - USE `Short Stick` with cheese (`Cheese`)

## 7. Entrance Hallway

- EXIT right

## 8. Kitchen

- TAKE pitcher (`Pitcher`)

## 9. Basement

- USE trunk
  - ENTER `312` (`Wire`, `Blue Chalk`)
- USE `Sandwich` with rathole
- USE `Toast with jam` with rathole
- USE `Cheese` with rathole
- LOOK AT rathole
- TAKE drill (`Drill`)
- USE `Short Stick` with `Drill` (`Drill with Handle`)
- USE `Drill with Handle` with barrel (`Pitcher with Oil`)
- USE `Pitcher with Oil` with engine
- USE `Wire` with engine
- USE machine (`Empty Bottle`)

## 10. Basement

- USE the three levers to switch them in the following positions:
  - Red: middle
  - Yellow: bottom
  - Blue: top
- Then change alle three switches to the bottom position (`Diamond`)
- TAKE ball (`Ball`)
- USE tank lid (from the water tank)
- USE switch (near the door)
  - **NOTE:** How the direction of air changes
- USE `Apple` with hole (left of the switch)
- USE switch
- USE `Empty Bottle` with water tank (`Bottle of Water`)
- USE `Wrapping Paper` with machine
- USE `Blue Chalk` with machine (`Rough Sketch`)
- LOOK AT unknown device (the brown machine)
- USE `Ball` with replicator
- USE `Rough Sketch` with replicator
- USE replicator (`Piece of Machine`)
- USE `Piece of Machine` with machine
- USE `Diamond` with machine
- USE `Bottle of Water` with engine
- USE machine
