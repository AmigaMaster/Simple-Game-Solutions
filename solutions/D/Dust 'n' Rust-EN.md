## 2. Junkyard

## 3. The Ship

## 4. Junkyard

## 5. Bar

- TALK TO bartender
  - **Riddle:** Moonshine Roulette
    1. Grippotak (Green bottle on the top shelf)
    2. Local brewed swamp juice (Yellow bottle on the top shelf)
    3. Washammalow 65 (Purple bottle on the left of the bottom shelf)
    4. Grokstew (Big brown bottle in the middle of the bottom shelf)
    5. Listerine Cool Mint (Blue bottle on the right of the bottom shelf) (`Trophy`)

## 6. Junkyard

- TALK TO Joe
  - "Give him the trophy" (`Signed album`)

## 7. The Ship

- TALK TO robot
  - "Give the robot the signed album"
