# Quest for Yeti - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author                                    |
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------|
|   1.0   | 25.10.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de) |

Download the free game from [Adventure Game Studio](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/play/game/972-quest-for-yeti)

## 1. Southwestern Forest

- EXIT east

## 2. Southern Forest

- PICK UP lamp (`Lamp`)
- EXIT east

## 3. Polar Bear

- TALK TO Polar Bear
  - "Silly me, polar bears don't speak the Queen's English."
  - "Why not partake of a crash diet?" (`Fishing Rod`)
- EXIT north **2 times**, west **2 times**

## 4. Ocean

- USE `Fishing Rod` on various areas of the ocean (`Fish`
  - There are three hidden spots where you catch a fish, you only need to find one:
    1. Move the cursor in between the second and third lower wave in the lower left corner. The yellow hook of the fishing rod must be above the middle curvature.
    2. Between the top left two waves. The fishing rod hook must be above the left curvature of the second wav from the top
    3. Between the topmost wave and the one below. The fishing rod hook must be above the middle curvature of the lower wave
- EXIT east **3 times**

## 5. Penguin

- USE `Fish` on penguin
- PICK UP dynamite (`Dynamite`)
- EXIT west **2 times**, south, west **2 times**

## 6. Cracked Boulder

- USE `Dynamite` on boulder
- PICK UP pickaxe (`Pickaxe`)
- EXIT east **2 times**

## 7. Ice Hole

- USE `Pickaxe` on hole
- PICK UP oil (`Oil`) (`Lit Lamp`)
- EXIT east

## 8. Tree

- PICK UP compass (`Compass`)

## 9. Cave Entrance

- ENTER cave

### 9.1. Cave

- LOOK map
  - **NOTE:** `Seen`
- USE `Compass` on map
  - **NOTE:** `SEEN` must be the directions you need to walk
- EXIT south, east **2 times**, north (through the trees)
