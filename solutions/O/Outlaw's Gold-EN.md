# Outlaw's Gold - Walkthrough

| Version | Date       | Author
|:-------:|------------|-------------------------------------------
|   1.0   | 24.07.2026 | Amiga Master (AmigaMaster-NG@t-online.de)

Download the free game from [Adventure Game Studio](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/play/game/2690-outlaw-s-gold)

## 1. Tutorial

- PICK UP big blue cup (`Blue Cup`)
- LOOK AT `Blue Cup`
- LOOK AT `Book`
- EXIT east

## 2. House

- TALK TO old man (`Map`)
- LOOK AT `Map`

## 3. Lake

- PICK UP thin rope (`Thin Rope`)

## 4. Mines

- USE box **2 times** (`Matches`, `Rusty Key`)

## 5. Hilltop

- USE `Rusty Key` on door
- PICK UP dynamite (`Dynamite`)
- USE `Thin Rope` on honey drop (`Sticky Rope`)

## 6. Mines

- USE `Sticky Rope` on tin can (`Homemade Fuse`)
- USE `Homemade Fuse` on `Dynamite` (`Explosives`)
- USE `Explosives` on obstruction
- USE `Matches` on explosives
- EXIT east

### 6.1. Mine Entrance

- LOOK AT generator

## 7. House

- LOOK AT jerrycan
- USE jerrycan
- TALK TO old man
  - "Ask about jerrycan" (`Fishing Rod`)

## 8. Lake

- USE `Fishing Rod` on try here (the hotspot between the two yellow  and one yellow flower on the right, above the single light green bush rounding) (`Boot`)

## 9. House

- USE `Boot` on old man (`Jerrycan`)

## 10. Mines

### 10.1. Mine Entrance

- USE `Jerrycan` on generator
